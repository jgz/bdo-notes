# Maegu upgrade-material crafting cheat sheet

> Last updated: 2026-08-23. The two stones Jon crafts to enhance his gear — both via **Processing (L)
> → Heating**. Recipes confirmed in-game ([Flawless Chaotic recipe screenshot](../../research/account/2026-08-23-flawless-chaotic-black-stone-recipe.jpg)).
> Both stones are **bound** (can't be bought — craft only). Enhancement details → [enhancement.md](enhancement.md).

## 🛡️ ARMOR (Slumbering Origin: Fallen God's / Labreska / Dahn / Ator) → **Flawless Chaotic Black Stone**

Heat **per stone** — two recipes, **same net mats**, use whichever you have on hand:
| Recipe A (direct — simplest) | Recipe B (if you already have FMBS) |
|---|---|
| **Sharp Black Crystal Shard ×2** | **Flawless Magical Black Stone ×1** |
| **Mass of Pure Magic ×1** | **Caphras Stone ×10** |
| **Caphras Stone ×10** | |

_(A Flawless Magical Black Stone is itself Sharp Black Crystal Shard ×2 + Mass of Pure Magic ×1, so
Recipe B is just Recipe A with an extra step — use A unless you have leftover FMBS from Blackstar days.)_

## 🔟 Mass Process (do this — 10 at a time)

Both stones: **10× the materials + 1 Black Stone Powder** per batch → **10 stones at once.** Always
mass-process; it's far faster than one at a time.

**No mastery proc (fixed yield).** Unlike cooking/alchemy (where guru mastery procs bonus output),
these enhancement-stone recipes are **fixed** — Processing Mastery does **not** give extra stones. A
10-batch yields exactly 10. _(Strong understanding — if mastery procced free black stones it'd be a
famous exploit, and it isn't. Easy self-check: if a 10-batch ever yields 11+, mastery is proccing.)_
Jon's Master processing still helps elsewhere (intermediate mats, life-skill income), just not here.

## The bottlenecks (what to stockpile / farm)

1. **Caphras** — 10 per stone, in *both* recipes. The universal sink. Buy/grind in bulk.
2. **Mass of Pure Magic** — the one armor material you **can't buy** — farm it (1 per armor stone).
3. **Primordial Crystal** — weapons only; from LoML boss tokens (already sorted).

Everything else (Sharp Black Crystal Shard, Black Stone Powder) is cheap on the market — just buy.

## Reminders

- **Fails degrade the gear** (armor −1 level & **−30 max durability**; weapon −20) — repair max
  durability with **Memory Fragments** (use **Artisan's Memory** = ×5). See [enhancement.md](enhancement.md).
- **Weapon fail-protection:** **Primordial Hammer** (Sovereign, TRI+, no FS gain) or **Cron** (any
  level, builds FS: PRI 320/DUO 560/TET 970/PEN 1350). **J's Hammer does NOT work on Sovereign.**
  A Primordial Hammer melts to 1,000 Crons. (Armor protection = **Crystal of Origin** items.)
- Meta: take **armor to DUO** cheaply, revisit TRI+ later with Crons + high failstacks; TET→PEN is brutal.
