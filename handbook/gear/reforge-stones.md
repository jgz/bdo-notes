## What it is

**Sovereign/Primordial weapons have 5 reforge slots.** You slot a **Reforge Stone** into each for a
flat stat bonus — **5 unique effects per weapon, no duplicates** (each effect once). This is on top of
enhancement and crystals. Three Sovereign weapons × 5 slots = **15 reforges** to fill long-term.

## How to apply

**Black Spirit → Item Reform → Reforge.** Pick the Sovereign weapon (top-right list), choose a
Reforge Stone, press **Reforge**. The stones **don't need enhancing** — they just slot in.

**⚠ Permanent:** a Reforge Stone **can't be extracted** once used — you don't get it back. So pick
deliberately. _(Overwrite rules are worded ambiguously across guides — verify in-game before assuming
you can freely swap a filled slot.)_

## Effects & the PvE loadout

Standard effects include: **All AP +3, All Accuracy +6, All Damage Reduction +5, All Evasion +10,
Max HP +200, Crit Rate +5%, Crit Damage +1%, positional/monster damage, BSR Recovery +0.5%.** There
are also **HAN** (enhanced) versions with better values (e.g. HAN All AP +5) — crafted via **Heating**
(5 identical standard stones + Nev's Fragment + Caphras + Magical Shards + Magical Lightstone Crystals).

**Recommended PvE 5-slot loadout** (per weapon): **All AP · All Accuracy · Extra Monster AP (species
damage) · All Damage Reduction · + Max HP or Crit Damage.** Upgrade to HAN versions later for more.
