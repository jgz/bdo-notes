**The shared money sink is Caphras** — both crafted stones need **10 Caphras each**. So Caphras is
the material to stockpile above all else.

## Armor — all four pieces, same system

The four armor pieces share one enhancement system and one **Agris Essence pity pool**. Path:
Base → **PRI (Desperate)** → **DUO (Distorted)** → **TRI (Silent)** → **TET (Wailing)** → **PEN (Obliterating)**.

**Per attempt: 1× Flawless Chaotic Black Stone** — *player-crafted only* via **Processing → Heating**:
- **Sharp Black Crystal Shard ×2 + Caphras ×10 + Mass of Pure Magic ×1**, or
- **Caphras ×10 + Flawless Magical Black Stone ×1**
- (add **Black Stone Powder ×1** to craft **×10** at once)

**Fail-protection: Crystal of Origin** items (instead of Cron) protect specific steps —
**Desperate** (PRI→DUO), **Distorted** (DUO→TRI), **Silent** (TRI→TET). From the **Manage Currency**
exchange + drops in **Nymphamaré / Orbita Castle** (Edania/LoML grind zones).

**Agris pity** (guaranteed success at the threshold, +1 per fail, shared across all 4 pieces):
Base→PRI **12** · PRI→DUO **15** · DUO→TRI **20** · TRI→TET **50** · TET→PEN **1000**.

> **Meta advice (BDFoundry):** take armor to **DUO cheaply**, then **stop** and come back for **TRI+
> later with Crons + very high failstacks**. TET→PEN is brutal (the 1000-Agris pity = a tiny base
> rate). Don't brute-force it early. _(BDFoundry only shows the recommended per-tier failstacks as a
> chart image, not text — so no exact TET→PEN number to quote yet.)_

**Material sources:** Caphras (market / grind) · Sharp Black Crystal Shard (market / grind) · **Mass
of Pure Magic (NOT marketable** — grinding, field/world bosses, gathering magical/mysterious seeds,
Star's End excavation, or the [Weekly] Delivering Essence of Tunkuta quest) · Flawless Magical Black
Stone (self-heat) · Black Stone Powder (cheap, market).

## Sovereign weapons — main / awakening / sub

Path: base → PRI → DUO → TRI → TET → **PEN** → HEX → SEP → OCT → NOV → DEC (post-PEN is a long grind).

**Per attempt: 1× Primordial Black Stone** — buy on the Central Market, or heat it:
**Primordial Crystal ×1 + Sharp Black Crystal Shard ×2 + Caphras ×10**.

- **Primordial Crystal** (the catalyst) — **marketable**; also Night Vendor (Patrigio) + a weekly
  world-boss quest (LoML/Seoul boss — where the boss alts help).
- **Degrades on fail** (−20 max durability). **Cron protects** the level: PRI 320 / DUO 560 / TET 970
  / **PEN 1,350** Crons. **J's Hammer can't be used.** Agris pity also applies.
- **TET→PEN base rate: 0.469% @ 0 FS → ~7.5% @ 150 FS.** So: **150+ failstacks + Crons**, or grind the
  Agris pity. Hardest normal step before the HEX–DEC endgame.
