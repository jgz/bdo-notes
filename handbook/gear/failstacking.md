# Failstacking — and why Jon barely needs to fear it

> Last updated: 2026-08-23. Failstacking is the thing that historically kept Jon out of enhancement.
> Good news: his **100k Cron stockpile** makes it far less of a wall than it looks.

## What a failstack is (30 seconds)

Each **failed** enhancement adds **+1** to your failstack; a higher failstack = higher success chance
on your next tap. Failstacks are **per-character** and are **consumed when an enhancement succeeds**.
So you "build up" a number by failing cheap stuff, then "spend" it on the real enhancement.

## Jon's reality: you're far less failstack-limited than you think

**Cron-protected fails BUILD your failstack for free** (Cron protects the *level*, not the stack — a
protected fail still gives +1 and no downgrade). With ~100k Crons that means:

- **Seed with one Advice of Valks, then Cron-tap.** Pop a mid Advice (say +100) to set a floor, then
  protect-tap your real gear — every fail bumps the stack (+101, +102…) with **zero downgrade risk**
  until it lands. You only spend **one Advice per run**, not one per tap.
- **Your Advice pile seeds tons of runs** (2026-08-23): +30 ×161, +40 ×115, +60 ×53, +50 ×29, +20 ×45,
  plus +80 ×10, +100 ×11, +120 ×5. Loads of mid stacks.
- **Your scarce high ones** — +150 ×3, +180 ×3, +200 ×2, +250, +300 ×2, +350 ×1, +450 ×1 — **save for
  the precious weapon taps** (below).

## When high stacks actually matter

- **Armor (Fallen God's/Labreska/Dahn/Ator)** — mats are plentiful, so **don't sweat it**: seed a
  +60–100 Advice and Cron-tap. Wasting a few cheap Flawless Chaotic stones costs nothing.
- **Weapons (Sovereign)** — each Primordial Black Stone is gated by a **Primordial Crystal** from
  limited LoML boss spawns, so stones are *precious*. Here you want a **high stack (250–300+) BEFORE
  each tap** so you don't burn scarce stones on low-odds attempts. Use your high Advice, buy more, or
  hand-build (below).

## Topping up: buy Advice of Valks with currencies

Many shops sell Advice of Valks for coins/currencies (the **Olvia coin shop** had +200/+250; also
loyalty/event/Pearl shops, Fughar, Challenge (Y) tab). Easy, no-skill top-up — watch for them.

## Hand-building (the Reblath method) — optional for Jon

The classic cheap way to build a big stack without touching real gear:
1. Buy cheap **Reblath Shoes** from **Tranan Underfoe (Velia)**.
2. Enhance the Reblath piece to **+14** (best fail rate while only using cheap Black Stone (Armor)).
3. Tap **+14 → +15** with **Black Stone (Armor)** — it fails nearly every time, building your stack cheaply.
4. On a success (+15), reset it and keep going.
5. **Store the built stack in Naderr's Band** so a lucky success elsewhere doesn't waste it.

**Optional for Jon** given the Cron surplus — but it's the way to bank a big stack cheaply before a
precious weapon PEN tap. It's not complicated: fail cheap stuff → number goes up → spend it.

## Bottom line

The thing that scared you off enhancement is mostly solved by your Cron pile: **protect-tap and let
the stacks build on the real gear.** Reserve hand-building/high Advice for the scarce weapon stones.
