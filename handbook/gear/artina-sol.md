# Artina Sol upgrade path (from PEN Tuvala Sol)

> Last updated: 2026-08-22. Full path for Jon's eventual **buff-bot Shai** (Shaijeezy), starting from
> **PEN (V) Tuvala Sol**. Sourced: BDFoundry Sunset Artina Sol guide (2026-07-31), orbit-games, BDO
> Codex, official NA/EU update history; Garmoth via snippets (fetch-blocked). Quantities consistent
> across 4 sources.

## 🎉 The good news: your PEN carries all the way through

Every step **keeps your enhancement level** and the reforms are **100% guaranteed** — so **no
re-enhancing, no black stones, no Cron, no degrade risk.** You're just collecting **two "glow
crystals"** and applying them. The whole thing is a quest exchange + two guaranteed reforms.

**The catch is the materials, not the risk** — specifically two things to stockpile: **10,000 Caphras**
and **100 Embers of the Primordial.** Both overlap with what Jon's *already* gathering for Maegu, so
the same farming feeds this (but also competes — see below).

## The 3 steps

### 1. PEN Tuvala Sol → PEN Artina Sol (free exchange quest)
- **Quest `[Sol Exchange] PEN (V) Artina Sol`** from **Fughar** — **once per Family**, Shai only (added
  the 2026-07-30 patch). Prereq: the Shai Talent questline `[Shai Talent] The Musician of Light, Shai`.
- **Straight exchange — keeps PEN.** Hand in the PEN Tuvala Sol ×1, get PEN Artina Sol at the same
  level. No fee, no black stones. ⚠ It **deletes any item brand** on the Tuvala Sol and consumes it.

### 2. Artina Sol → Sunset Artina Sol (+35 Extra Monster AP)
- **Black Spirit → Item Reform → apply a Sunset Glow Crystal.** 100% guaranteed, keeps PEN.
- **Sunset Glow Crystal is CRAFTED** (Processing → Heating), **not** buyable:

| Material | Qty | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Remnants of the Rift | 1 | CM / Dark Rift |
| Forest Fury | 20 | CM / forest grind |
| Magical Shard | 300 | CM / heat black magic crystals |
| **Caphras Stone** | **10,000** | CM / grind — **the big cost** |

### 3. Sunset → Primordial Artina Sol (endgame: +10 more = +45 total, + 5 Reforge slots)
- **Item Reform → apply a Primordial Glow Crystal.** 100% guaranteed, keeps PEN. Also unlocks the **5
  Reforge Stone slots** ([reforge-stones](reforge-stones.md)).
- **Primordial Glow Crystal is CRAFTED** (Heating):

| Material | Qty | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Magical Shard | 20 | CM |
| Sharp Black Crystal Shard | 50 | CM / grind |
| **Flame of the Primordial** | 1 | Craft (below) |

- **Flame of the Primordial** (Heating) = **Embers of the Primordial ×100** — **bound, not on CM.**
  Farm from **LoML world bosses** (seal → 50 Embers) + **Olvia Academy Coins** (→ 50).

## Stockpile these two (overlaps with Maegu farming)

1. **10,000 Caphras** — Caphras is *the* universal sink (Maegu's armor + weapons already eat 10 per
   crafted stone). Grinding **Olun's Valley** under **Agris Fever** covers all of it → [enhancement](enhancement.md).
2. **100 Embers of the Primordial** — Jon's **LoML boss alts + Olvia coins** already farm these for
   Maegu's Sovereign weapons, so they're accumulating — **but the Sol competes with the weapons for
   the same Embers**, so budget for both.

## "One night to knock it out"?

Yes — **once you have the mats.** The exchange + two reforms are quick and guaranteed (an evening's
work). The *long* part is gathering the **10k Caphras + 100 Embers** beforehand — that's the thing to
start hoarding now and check what he already has.
