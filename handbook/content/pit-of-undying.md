# Pit of the Undying — daily boss rush

> Last updated: 2026-08-22. Jon's accessory-enhancement log (for Kharazad) routes through the Pit
> questline at chapter 2. Sourced: [Garmoth](https://garmoth.com/guides/post/pit-of-the-undying),
> [BDFoundry](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/pit-of-undying-crows-nest-questline/), official wiki.

## The key facts

- **No boat needed after unlock.** The one-time unlock is the **Crow's Nest** questline (the misty
  island). After that you **teleport in daily via ESC Menu → War → Pit of the Undying.** So there's
  **no reason to park an alt** out there.
- **Daily solo boss-rush arena.** Difficulty tiers **PRI → DUO → TRI → TET → PEN**, unlocked by
  promotion quests; rewards scale up per tier.
- **Run it on Maegu** (328 AP / GS 714) — good gear = higher tiers = better rewards. Built to be
  accessible from Lv56, so the lower tiers are trivial for Jon.

## Why it's worth doing

1. **Feeds the Kharazad accessory-enhancement log** — chapter 2 requires the Pit questline, and the
   content supplies accessory-enhancement items. Doing it regardless.
2. **Undying Crow's Insignia — up to +30% Combat EXP.** Permanent combat-EXP boost; **stacks with tag
   XP-sharing** ([tagging](../account/tagging.md)) to level alts faster.
3. **~100M silver/day** at high difficulty (100 Seals/day → Fine Accessory Box II) + **Crow's Black
   Jewels** for full clears. Seals of the Undying exchange for rewards.

## Add to routine

Once unlocked, it's a quick **daily teleport** → run the boss rush → collect Seals. Good candidate for
the [daily routine](../daily-routine.md) while Jon wants the EXP insignia + accessory items + silver.
