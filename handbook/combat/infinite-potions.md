# Infinite Potions (Ornette's / Odore's Spirit Essence)

> Last updated 2026-08-25. The never-ending HP/MP potions Jon has been chipping at for years. The big
> change since he last played is the **pity system** (2021 overhaul) — the grind is now **guaranteed**,
> not pure RNG, and Jon's **Atanis' Element** stockpile converts straight into pity progress. Sourced:
> BDFoundry Infinite Potions guide + NA patch history; item IDs via bdo-cmd. ⚠️ verify exchange NPCs/
> counts in-client (they shift by patch).

## What they are / why finish them

- **Ornette's Spirit Essence** = **infinite HP potion** (550 HP, same stats/cooldown as an instant XL).
- **Odore's Spirit Essence** = **infinite MP/WP/EP/SP potion** (works for every class's resource).
- **Never deplete, cost 0 silver forever, weigh ~50 LT total** (vs ~20 XL potions of weight) — saves
  billions over time and frees inventory/weight. Once crafted, drop them in **Family Inventory** and use
  across all characters. This is a permanent account QoL win — worth closing out.

## The 5 pieces per potion

Each potion = **combine 5 component pieces**. First 3 are the grind/pity ones; last 2 are resource
exchanges unlocked after the first 3.

| # | **HP** (Ornette's) | **MP** (Odore's) |
|--:|---|---|
| 1 | Sherekhan's Panacea | Valtarra's Clairvoyance |
| 2 | Ron's Tintinnabulum | Markthanan's Gland |
| 3 | Ash Halfmoon Kagtunak | Narc's Crimson Tear |
| 4 | Gayak's Courage Stone *(exchange)* | Krogdalo's Protection Stone *(exchange)* |
| 5 | Musical Spirit's Sound Stone *(exchange)* | Night Crow's Dawn Stone *(exchange)* |

## The pity system (THE change — grind is now guaranteed)

For each of the **first 3 pieces**: collect **100 pity pieces** → trade them to **NPC Merindora (Grana)**
for the matching **catalyst** ("roar/breath" item) → **Simple Alchemy: 100 pity pieces + catalyst = the
full piece.** (e.g. 100 Dragon's Fang + 1 Dragon's Roar = Sherekhan's Panacea.) No more praying for a
~50-hour RNG drop.

**Three ways to get pity pieces (stack them):**
1. **⭐ Atanis' Element — Jon's stockpile.** **1 Atanis' Element = 1 pity piece**, exchanged at
   **Merindora (Grana)** / via Manage Currency, for **any** infinite-potion pity piece. This is Jon's
   fast path — spend the banked Elements straight into whichever first-3 components he still needs.
   (Atanis' Element drops in certain monster zones; the **Season server also grants 100 once per
   family**.)
2. **Weekly quests** — **+5 pity pieces each**, reset **Thursday 00:00 UTC**. HP: Sherekhan Necropolis
   (1,000 kills) · Forest Ronaros (1,000) · Blood Wolf Settlement (1,500). MP: Navarn Steppe (250 kills +
   250 feathers) · Tshira Ruins (1,500) · Manshaum Forest (1,000).
3. **Direct grinding** — the pity pieces drop from those same designated zones while you grind.

## The last 2 pieces (resource exchange, after the first 3)

Not pity-based — hand in materials at node managers:
- **HP:** Gayak's Courage Stone = **30 Garmoth's Scales** (Camira) · Musical Spirit's Sound Stone = **300
  Peridot Leaves** (Moyamo).
- **MP:** Krogdalo's Protection Stone = **100 Rumbling Earth Shards** · Night Crow's Dawn Stone = **100
  Dragon Scale Fossils** (Forboding Altar).

## Final craft

Once all 5 pieces are in hand: use **Yaz's Combinables Pouch**, arrange the 5 in a **+ shape**, combine →
the infinite potion. (Or the incremental Blessed → Greater Blessed → full path.)
