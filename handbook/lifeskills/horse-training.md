## Two misconceptions to clear first

1. **Doomeezy (T10 Mythical) is NOT your wagon "carry."** A max-level horse on a wagon **wastes a
   slot** — wagon Mount EXP is **split across the hitched horses**, so a finished horse just absorbs a
   share that should go to one that still needs levels (BDFoundry: *"max-level horses don't accelerate
   others' progression"*). On top of that, guides uniformly say **never wagon-train Dream/Mythical
   horses** (wagons nullify horse-costume/Krogdalo set effects). So Doomeezy stays a personal mount —
   he's never on a training team.
2. **T8 → T9 is ONE courser, not two.** You don't combine two coursers for a Dream Horse. You awaken a
   single trained courser **repeatedly** (it survives failures). The "two same-type horses" rule is
   only the **T9 → T10 Mythical** step.

## How training works (quick)

- **Mount EXP scales with distance travelled** → a **faster horse earns more EXP/hour**. Speed gear
  (horseshoe / wagon wheel) directly raises training speed.
- **Level caps:** T1–7 cap at **Lv 30**; **T8, T9 Dream, T10 Mythical go to Lv 100**. A fresh T8 to
  Lv 30 ≈ **40–50 hrs of AFK**.
- **Skill learning:** each level-up is a *chance* to learn a mount skill. Your **Training life-skill
  level raises that chance** — at **Training Guru 20 it's ~100%** (a skill almost every level). This is
  why **courser hunting is done SOLO** (you want every level to roll a skill toward the 7 courser
  skills).
- **Training Mastery** (from gear) boosts wild-capture rate, mount EXP, and — critically — **breeding /
  exchange tier chances** (higher mastery = T8 foals from T8 parents at lower combined parent levels).

**Stackable EXP buffs:** Value Pack (+30% Mount EXP), Secret Book of Old Moon (+50% Life EXP),
Trainer's Clothes (Loggia ~28% → Manos ~40% Training EXP at TET), Stonetail Wind's Meal, **Fiery
Celerity Draught** (+30/+30, 600 min), Manos accessory set (up to +65% Life EXP), Krogdalo horse gear
(speed/utility). _(Fairy training-EXP %, Peridot Leaf, and Lump of Raw Sugar are NOT confirmed training-XP
boosters — they tie to taming/breeding/processing; don't count on them for XP.)_

## The overnight AFK setup

Set a **looped auto-path** (right-click map → *Set Looped Auto-Path*, Alt+RMB checkpoints, press **T**),
keep the loop **as straight as possible** (avoid rocks/mobs/obstacles), tick **auto-consume feed** so
the horse **auto-eats carrots** — use **Carrot Confit** (restores stamina *and* HP) so it loops
**indefinitely** overnight. **Stonetail Horse Ranch** is the standard loop hub. Match the method to
the goal:

| Goal | Method | Team |
|---|---|---|
| **Bulk-level MANY horses** (breeding fodder, T1–T8) | **Forest Path Wagon** | **4 horses that all still need levels** — no max horses, no coursers-in-progress, no Dream horses |
| **One specific horse** (Courser hunt, or level a Dream/Mythical to 100) | **SOLO auto-loop** | just that horse (keeps costume set effects + max per-level skill rolls) |

- **Forest Path Wagon** hitches **4 horses and trains all 4 at once**; it's the best AFK wagon
  (permanent, won't break). **Turn Forest Rush OFF overnight** (it can snag the wagon). Wagon = more
  *total* EXP but split 4 ways; **solo = far faster for one horse**.
- Overnight buffs: Value Pack + Fiery Celerity Draught + best Trainer's Clothes + Stonetail Wind's Meal.

## The pipeline: breed → T8 Courser → T9 Dream Horse

**The ladder, in order:**
1. **Breed up to a Tier 8.** Register male + female at a Stable Keeper's Breeding Market (~2.24 hrs →
   offspring egg, can be higher tier than parents). **Breed counts:** male **2**, female **1**; when
   both hit 0, use **Exchange** (35k silver, instant, **destroys both parents** → one offspring) to
   recycle into a higher-tier shot. Offspring tier depends on **parent levels + your Training Mastery**.
2. **Courser awaken** the T8 → learn **all 7 courser skills** (Charge, Sprint, Drift, Instant Accel,
   Sideways, S:Instant Accel, S:Sideways) + reach **Lv 30**. It auto-becomes a Courser (gold icon).
3. **Courser Training** that ONE courser to **200%** across Skill / Elegance / Strength (green mats
   +1%, blue +2%, each stat caps 180%).
4. **Awaken → chance at a T9 Dream Horse.** At **NPC Gula (Stonetail)** or **Melabee (Grána)**. Base
   **1% + 0.2%/failstack**; **~30 Agris stacks guarantees** it. **Cron Stones** make a fail drop you to
   100% training (half refill) instead of a full wipe. Each attempt eats **1 Krogdalo's Origin Stone**
   + the mats to refill to 200%. The **courser survives failures** — expect **~15–35 attempts**.
   - **Which type you get is steered by your highest stat:** **Skill → Arduanatt**, **Elegance → Diné**,
     **Strength → Doom**.
5. **T9 → T10 Mythical** (much later): **two same-type Lv-30 T9s** (M+F) + a **Mythical Censer**, base
   **3% +0.2%/fail**; success consumes both, failure returns both. All three Mythicals → **Krogdalo's
   Sanctuary**.

**For Jon's Krogdalo goal specifically:** he's getting **T10 Doom (Doomeezy) free**, and has a **T9
Arduanatt** already. So the training grind needs to produce: a **2nd T9 Arduanatt** (to pair for a T10

Dream Horses via the courser-awakening grind — a genuine long-haul, which is fine as an overnight

## Imperial Horse Delivery — the reject sink

Turn unwanted foals/horses into value: Stable Keeper → **Stable → Check In** → select horse →
**Imperial Horse Delivery** (horse must be **Lv 15+**).

- **Pays ~50% of market value** (scales with tier/level/skills) — e.g. a T8 Courser ≈ 800M via delivery
  vs ~1.6B on the market. **So never deliver a good courser** (list it instead); deliver genuine
  dead-ends only.
- **Golden Seal – [Imperial Training]:** you get seals = the horse's **TIER** (T8 = 8 seals; level
  doesn't matter). Seals buy training gear/mats (Granverre/Manos gear, Powder of Ascension, etc.).
- **Flower of Oblivion:** scales by tier (1 @ T1 → **20 @ T8**). You need **100 per T10 Mythical
  attempt** — so Imperial-Delivering high-tier rejects **banks Flowers for the Krogdalo grind**. Nice
  synergy: the horses that don't make courser feed the mythical attempts.
