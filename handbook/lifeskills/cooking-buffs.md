# Cooking session buffs — the stack for long cooking runs

> Last updated 2026-08-26. What to eat/drink/equip for long Balenos/Imperial cooking sessions. Two levers
> matter: **cook time** (meals/hour) and **mastery** (yield/craft — see [mastery-yield.md](mastery-yield.md));
> plus **weight** (session length) and **Life EXP** (growing mastery). Effects + prices from the NA client
> dump + bdo-cmd (2026-08-26). These buffs come from **separate slots and all stack** — 1 draught + 1
> food + the alchemy stone + worn/socketed gear at once.

## ⭐ The key realization for Jon

**The cooking buffs largely solve your weight bottleneck during a session.** Viridian Draught **+300 LT**
+ Seafood Cron Meal **+100 LT** + Ancient Book of Life **+250 LT** = up to **+650 temporary LT** the
moment you buff up. So "cooking is weight-gated" mostly applies to *un-buffed* weight — buffed, you have a
lot more room. (Permanent weight still worth raising → [weight-limit.md](weight-limit.md).)

## The stack (one from each slot — they stack)

### 1. Draught (elixir slot) — pick one
| Draught | Cook time | Also gives | Price / duration |
|---|--:|---|---|
| **⭐ Viridian Draught** (800) | **−3 sec** | +25 Life Mastery · **+300 LT** · +25% Processing · +30% Life EXP · +3 gather/move/fish | ~8M · 15 min |
| Verdure Draught (791) — budget | −1 sec | +200 LT · +20% Processing · +20% Life EXP | ~1.3M · 15 min |

Viridian is the premium all-in-one (cook time **and** weight **and** mastery). At ~8M/15min (~32M/hr) it
sounds steep, but Imperial cooking nets ~147M/day — for a big batch session the speed-up pays for itself.
Verdure or the food-only route for casual/sustained cooking.

### 2. Food (food slot) — pick one (you can COOK all of these)
| Food | Cook time | Also gives | Price / duration |
|---|--:|---|---|
| **Seafood Cron Meal** (9691) | −0.6 sec | +100 LT · +25 Life Mastery · +10% Processing · +10% Life EXP | ~650k · 120 min |
| Savory Cron Meal (9695) — mastery focus | — | +25 Life Mastery · +100 LT · +5% Life EXP | 120 min |
| Teff Sandwich (9218) — cheap throughput | −0.5 sec | — | ~49k · 120 min |

Seafood Cron Meal is the best all-round cooking food (time + mastery + weight). Teff Sandwich is the
throwaway-cheap option if you just want cook time.

### 3. Equipped — Alchemy Stone of Life (press to activate)
Persistent while active; grades: Imperfect −0.5s → Rough −0.7 → Polished −0.9 → Sturdy −1.1 → **Sharp
−1.4s** cook time (+ up to +14% Processing). Alchemy-made (not on market). Use the best grade you have.

### 4. Worn — Cook's Clothes
**Manos / Loggia / Roroju Cook's Clothes** raise **Cooking Mastery**; the **Silver Embroidered** versions
also cut cook time. (Not weight — that myth is handled in [weight-limit.md](weight-limit.md).)

### 5. Socketed — Vital Crystal: Cooking (15242)
**+10 Cooking Mastery**, permanent while socketed.

### 6. Cooking utensil (in the residence)
Use **Advanced (3719)** or **Supreme (3743) Cooking Utensil** = **−1 sec**. ⚠️ **AVOID the "Traditional"
utensils** (Balenos +5s / Serendia +6s / Calpheon +7s) — they *slow you down*. Check which type the Olvia
Academy free utensil is before relying on it for speed.

### 7. Optional — mastery/EXP push
- **Ancient Book of Life** (7-day, 616409): +50% Life EXP · +25 Life Mastery · +250 LT · 60 min. Great
  during a big cook-and-level session.
- **Old Moon Miracle Mix** (617245): **+100 Life Skill Mastery** — event/Olvia item; the single biggest
  mastery boost if you have one.

## TL;DR loadout
**Big session:** Viridian Draught + Seafood Cron Meal + Sharp Alchemy Stone of Life + Cook's Clothes +
Vital Crystal: Cooking + Advanced/Supreme utensil (+ Ancient Book of Life if pushing mastery). Cook-time
reductions stack down toward the minimum craft time; mastery bonuses stack for more yield per cook.
**Cheap/sustained:** Verdure Draught (or skip) + Seafood Cron Meal + Teff Sandwich swap + the alchemy
stone + gear.
