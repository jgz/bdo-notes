# Royal Workshop (Gyeongbok Palace Management) — LoML Part 2 / Seoul

> Researched 2026-08-28 for "is the palace worker content worth my spare CP?" (Jon had 53 free CP).
> Sources: [BDFoundry Royal Workshop guide](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/royal-workshop-guide/) ·
> [BDFoundry LoML Seoul patch guide](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/land-of-the-morning-light-seoul-patch-guide/) ·
> [Official Adventurer's Guide: Royal Workshop](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-us/Wiki?wikiNo=424).

**What it is:** a Seoul-exclusive worker production/processing side-system in Gyeongbok Palace. You invest in
workshops (Palace Kitchen, Leftward/Rightward Office, Military, Infirmary…), assign workers, and they make
exclusive trade goods you sell — plus a low chance at rare buff foods.

## Verdict for Jon: worth it ✅ (cheap CP, fits the lifeskill profile)
Low-risk with spare CP, suits a worker-empire/guru-lifeskill account. Go in expecting a **daily-touch
side-hobby with lumpy payoff**, not a passive silver faucet.

## Cost
- **5 CP** to unlock the Royal Workshop (the only hard number the guides give).
- Plus the **Yukjo Street node** connection + ~**8 worker lodgings** (modest, standard worker-empire CP).
- Total comfortably inside 53 free CP.

## What you get
| Reward | Detail |
|---|---|
| Trade goods | ~**114k–301k each** to Trade Manager / Heo Seungwon (Buncheong Ware ~301k). Trade distance + Trading level apply. Modest steady income. |
| **Rare buff foods** (the real draw) | Low chance on daily refresh: **Bountiful Feast** (+5 AP, +1000% Combat EXP), **Assorted Kimchi** (+250% Life EXP, +100 LT). Sell **50M–200M** *and* directly useful. |
| Rare materials | Low chance → craft into rare processed trade goods. |

## Workers & management
- Workers come **exclusively from Yukjo Street node**. **Dokkebi** preferred (~24h on rare items vs ~36h
  Shellfolk). ~8 workers is enough.
- **Moderately active, not afk:** refresh production daily (free once/day, then 50 pearls per extra),
  reassign workers, process at processing workshops, haul + sell goods manually.
- Extra workshops unlocked via **Morning Gratitude Tokens** (500–1,000 each) earned by crafting — **not** CP.

## Notes / gaps
- No guide gives a clean silver/day figure — value is dominated by the **rare buff-food procs**, so income
  is spiky. Treat the steady trade-good sales as the floor.
- Separate from the general worker empire (uses Yukjo Street workers only) — doesn't conflict with the
  bdo-empire node optimization.
