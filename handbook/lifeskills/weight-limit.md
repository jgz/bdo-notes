## The one rule that shapes everything

**Character carry weight (LT) is PER-CHARACTER.** Each character earns its own. The *only* things that
apply account-wide are **Adventure Logs** and the **Value Pack** buff. So most of this list has to be
done **on JGZ specifically**.

⚠️ **Don't confuse two different "weights":**
- **Character Weight Limit (LT)** — the carry number on the character sheet. This is the cooking
  bottleneck. Items literally named **`Weight Limit +X LT`** (bound to character).
- **Family Inventory Weight** — a *separate shared storage bag*, family-wide, with its own questline.
  Items named **`Family Inventory Weight Limit +X LT`** (IDs 320xxx / 46930…). **Not** your cook's carry
  weight — don't chase these for cooking.

  cooking benefit. **Cook's Clothes = 0 LT** (speed only); **Manos/Loggia life clothes = 0 LT** (mastery
  only). So cook weight never comes from the outfit.

## How much is "enough"

| Tier | Total LT | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Getting by | ~700–900 | short semi-AFK sessions; refill often |
| **Comfortable (baseline)** | **~1,000–1,300** | long unattended cooking, easy Imperial hauling |
| Serious lifeskiller | ~1,500–2,000+ | "never think about weight" — needs the PEN belt |

Steepest value is base → ~1,000. Past ~1,300–1,500 it's convenience, not a bottleneck. Weight bites in
two places for cooking: hauling **ingredients** in bulk, and carrying **Guru's Cooking Boxes** (~1 LT
each) to the Imperial manager.
