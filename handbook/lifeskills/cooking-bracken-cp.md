# Stir-Fried Bracken — cooking-XP + CP-delicacy side recipe

> Jon mass-cooks this whenever he has spare time — it's a cheap **Professional-1** dish cooked for the
> **byproducts**, not the dish itself. Recipe/weights from bdo-cmd; batch sized to Jon's 1,600-LT bag.

## Recipe & max batch
Per craft: **8 Bracken · 5 Garlic · 2 Salt · 5 Mineral Water · 2 Olive Oil** (1.39 LT/craft, input-sized).

**Max batch = 1,151 crafts:**
| Ingredient | Pull |
|---|--:|
| Bracken | 9,208 |
| Garlic | 5,755 |
| Salt | 2,302 |
| Mineral Water | 5,755 |
| Olive Oil | 2,302 |

**Limiter = Garlic** — farm it (Magical Garlic Seed), see [farming](farming.md). Bracken is gathered/cheap.

## The byproducts (the whole point)
- **Witch's Delicacy** (9780) — cooking CP proc. **10 → 900 Contribution EXP + 30 Cooking EXP** at Olvia.
  Procs from ALL cooking + is the weekly cooking quest ([cp-and-passive-income](cp-and-passive-income.md)).
- **Savory Stir-Fried Bracken** (9319) — Master-1 higher-grade proc. **Sells well (~45k, heavy buyer
  demand) → sell these.** This is where the silver is.

## The normal Stir-Fried Bracken (9318) itself — low value
- **Uses:** Knowledge Gain Chance +5% buff food (60 min); ingredient in Special Drieghanese Meal;
  packages into Master's Cooking Box (imperial).
- **Market: oversupplied / effectively unsellable in bulk** — everyone self-makes it (tens of thousands
  listed vs a few hundred buyers). Don't try to dump a big stack.
- **Don't box it for imperial.** The daily imperial-cooking turn-in limit is shared, so a Master's Bracken
  box just displaces a **~800k Guru (Balenos) box** — net loss while Jon has Guru boxes to turn in.
- ⟹ Keep a stack as buff food; treat the rest as low-value byproduct. The value is the **Savory + Witch's
  Delicacy**, not the dish.
