## The 7 courser skills (reference)

A T8 must learn **all 7** of these to become a Courser (gold icon) and start awakening toward a
T9 Dream Horse: **Charge · Sprint · Drift · Instant Accel · Sideways · S: Instant Accel ·
S: Sideways**. Skills are rolled on level-up (RNG) and can be reset/cycled toward the missing
ones. The two `S:` skills are the usual bottleneck. _(Source:
[GrumpyG — Courser Skills & Training](https://grumpygreen.cricket/courser-skills-training/).)_

## Detail

### Scholar (T8, Lv 1)
- **Where it came from:** registered an item that was sitting on an alt Jon didn't know he had.
- **Courser progress: 4 of 7.** Already knows **Sprint, Instant Accel, S: Instant Accel,
  S: Sideways** — notably the two advanced `S:` skills, which are the ones people grind hardest
  for. Still needs **Charge, Drift, Sideways (base)**.
- **Verdict: yes, a viable courser candidate — a good one.** Level it **solo** (not on a wagon —
  wagons split EXP and you want the full per-level skill rolls) toward Lv 30, learning the
  remaining 3 skills as it goes; reset/cycle skills if the RNG stalls. Once it has all 7 it
  awakens toward a T9 Dream Horse. The 1 breed left is irrelevant to the courser path.
- **Caveat to verify in-game:** our older training note claims a horse's skill ceiling is fixed
  at birth (a dud can't learn all 7). GrumpyG says any T8 can get there via skill cycling
  ("Forechop Method"). Unresolved — but Scholar already having 4/7 including both `S:` skills
  makes it a low-risk bet either way.
