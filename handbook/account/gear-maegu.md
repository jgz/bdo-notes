## Read (observations — verify against current meta before acting)

The **weapons and body armor are in good shape** (PEN Blackstar primary, PEN Griffon + PEN Red
Nose), but there are clear soft spots that match Jon's "still gearing" description:

- **Awakening + sub-weapon are TET**, not PEN — PEN Blackstar Fans / PEN Kutum are the obvious
  weapon upgrades. Gloves are also only TET (Bheg's).
- **PEN Tuvala Shoes** are **leftover season gear** sitting in an otherwise boss-gear kit — a
  standing upgrade slot (to a real PEN boss/late-game boot).
- **Accessories are mostly Capotia** (Ring/Earring/Necklace/Belt) — the **free, non-enhanceable
  achievement tier** — plus one TET Crescent Guardian ring. This is the weakest axis and lines up
  with Jon's note that he was "still working on getting accessories to enhance." Real boss / current
  top-tier accessories are the biggest headroom here.

> **The answer to "what to upgrade" is Hyperboost.** The July-2026 catch-up system can lift Maegu
> from **281 → ~350 AP / 342 → ~427 DP largely for free** (Garmoth Upgrade Path, 2026-08-21):
> Capotia/Bronte's → **PEN Kharazad accessories**, armor → **Slumbering Origin**, weapons →
> **Sovereign**. See the plan: [../gear/hyperboost-plan.md](../gear/hyperboost-plan.md).
