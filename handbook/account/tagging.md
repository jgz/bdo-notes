# Character Tag + Item Copy — play a 2nd class on Maegu's gear

> Last updated: 2026-08-22. The tag system was overhauled since Jon last played (it used to cost a
> fortune). Sourced: [BDFoundry Tag guide](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/character-tag-guide-marnis-suspicious-device/),
> [GrumpyG](https://grumpygreen.cricket/tag/), official wiki (wikiNo=363).

## Why this matters for Jon

**Only the Sovereign WEAPONS are character-bound** — stuck on Maegu, can't be moved via storage. His
**armor + accessories (Kharazad, Fallen God's/Labreska/Dahn/Ator) are Family-bound** — he can just
**bank them and pull them out on any character** (confirmed by Jon 2026-08-22). So to play another
class on his Maegu gear he moves the armor/accessories via the bank, and handles **just the weapon**
via one of:
- a **Weapon Exchange Coupon** (moves the character-bound weapon to another character), or
- the **tag system's Item Copy** (copies the weapon onto the tagged char).

That's why the tag copy cost below is **weapon-based** — the weapon is the only piece that actually
needs copying.

## Current mechanics (2026)

- **Tagging is FREE** — connect **2 characters** (max 2 tagged at once); they share EXP.
- **Item Copy (gear copy) costs Marni's Unstable Fuel** (10M silver each, from the Old Moon Manager).
  - Cost is based on **weapons only** — armor copies free (armor can be swapped between chars anyway).
  - Formula ≈ *weapon Central Market value × 0.0015 + caphras count × 2,000 silver*. For character-bound
    top weapons (PEN Blackstar) the cost was effectively **capped ~1.5B**; **Sovereign** has no market
    price so it uses a reference value — **confirm the exact fuel cost in-game before copying.**
  - Copied gear stays on the tagged char until you untag it.
- **"Travel Separately"** toggle — tagged characters can be in **different locations** (leave one at a
  boss alt / a second grind spot) instead of sharing a location.

## The money-saver: wait for a copy-cost event

Pearl Abyss regularly runs events that **cut Item Copy cost by 50% or down to 1 fuel (10M total)**.
If Jon wants to copy his full Sovereign/Kharazad set onto a tagged alt, **do it during one of those
events** — it's then nearly free. (Watch the events list / this pairs with the [enhancement](../gear/enhancement.md)
event-watching habit.)

## Can't be copied

Jetina gear (unless PEN), Tuvala weapons after graduation, costumes, story-quest gear, rented CP gear.
Sovereign weapons are copyable (they're the point — armor/accessories move via bank anyway).
