# Boss alts — where to park them

> Last updated: 2026-08-22. Strategy note for Jon's parked boss-loot alts. Reasoning below;
> the one thing to verify in-game is flagged ⚠.

## The shift

Old **boss armor is devalued** — PEN Tuvala now matches PEN boss gear, and Slumbering Origin / new
armor has replaced it (per the Garmoth Hyperboost guide + the Hyperboost patch). So **alts parked at
the armor bosses** (Bheg, Giath, Dim Tree, Urugon, Red Nose, Griffon) are now **low value** — mostly
just black stones / caphras / memory frags.

Meanwhile the **Land of the Morning Light world bosses** (4 types, ~11–12 spawns/week each, fixed
schedule) drop **current, relevant loot**: **Embers of the Primordial, Primordial Crystal, Reforge
Stones (All AP / Accuracy / DR / …), and the rare Flame of the Primordial** — i.e. **Sovereign /
Primordial weapon enhance + reform materials.** That feeds the exact endgame weapon Jon is working
toward via Hyperboost. [BDFoundry](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/world-bosses-loml-guide/)

## The plan (Jon's call, 2026-08-22)

- **Keep an alt at Garmoth** — **Garmoth's Heart** still matters (crystal-slot item + Godr-Ayed mats).
- **Keep an alt at Vell** — **Vell's Heart** still valuable.
- **Relocate the Kzarka + Kutum alts** (weapon bosses — Kzarka main is a stepping stone below
  Blackstar/Sovereign; Kutum offhand keeps minor accuracy-build use) **to the LoML world bosses**, so
  they farm Sovereign mats instead of stale weapons.
- **Also prime to move:** any pure **armor-boss** alts (Bheg/Giath/Dim Tree/Urugon/etc.) — those are
  the most obsolete now.

## ⚠ Verify before committing

- **LoML world-boss reward structure:** confirm it's **per-character** (each parked alt earns the
  weekly Sovereign-mat rewards) vs a **per-Family weekly cap** (in which case extra alts don't help
  there and one character suffices). This decides whether relocating *multiple* alts actually
  multiplies the mats.
- **AP / contribution to get loot:** LoML bosses may need a minimum contribution/AP for a reward —
  make sure the relocated alts can hit it (they're low-gear alts).
