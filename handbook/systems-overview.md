## The two engines of a BDO account

BDO runs on two loosely-coupled progression engines. Jon's account is **strong on the second, weak
on the first** — that asymmetry drives the whole plan.

1. **Combat power** — your character's gear and levels, used to grind mobs for silver and access
   harder zones. Jon's weak axis.
2. **The life-skill / empire economy** — cooking, processing, gathering, workers, nodes, trading and
   the Central Market. Largely **AFK/idle or low-attention** income that runs alongside (or instead
   of) combat. Jon's strong axis.

Silver is the shared currency both engines feed and spend. The returning-player question is how to
use the strong engine to fund and shortcut the weak one.

## Combat progression

| System | What it does |
|---|---|
| Gear enhancement | Upgrade weapons/armor through tiers (…→ TRI/TET/PEN) via an RNG enhancement game. The core combat grind and the main silver sink. |
| Weapons | Main-hand, awakening, and sub-weapon, each on the enhancement ladder. Top tiers have moved over the years — **verify the current ceiling.** |
| Armor | Body/helmet/gloves/shoes plus set choices (evasion vs damage-reduction identities). |
| Accessories | Rings/earrings/necklace/belt — enhanced by combining duplicates, high-risk high-reward. |
| AP / DP brackets | Grind zones gate on attack/defense thresholds; hitting the next bracket is the usual gear goal. |
| Character level & skills | Levels, skill/awakening builds, and per-class specialization. |
| Catch-up gear | Season servers and event/quest gear that hand a fresh or returning account a big head start — **the likely fastest re-onboard; research current form.** |

## The life-skill economy — Jon's strength

| Life skill | Note for this account |
|---|---|
| **Cooking** | **Guru** — can run the daily guru-box loop; a known steady income and mastery sink. |
| **Processing** | **High Master tier** — chopping/heating/grinding/etc.; processing flips and mass-processing are classic life-skill income. |
| Gathering | Feeds processing and alchemy; mastery and tools matter. |
| Alchemy | Elixirs/oils; pairs with gathering/farming. |
| Trading | Trade goods, imperial delivery, and trade-route income. |
| Fishing | Low-attention/AFK income; changed over the years — verify. |
| Farming | Fences/crops feeding cooking and alchemy. |
| Sailing / Bartering | Ocean content — barter chains, sea trade, ship progression. |
| Hunting | Combat-flavored life skill; blue whales / matchlock content. |

Cross-cutting life-skill systems: **Life Mastery** (per-skill mastery score that boosts procs/yields,
raised by Manos gear + mastery accessories), **Energy** and **Contribution Points (CP)** as the
budgets that power gathering/nodes, and the **guru-box** daily loops at the top of cooking/alchemy.

## The empire — passive infrastructure

| System | What it does |
|---|---|
| Contribution Points (CP) | The budget spent to rent nodes, workers' lodging, and houses. Accumulated over years — a real asset to inventory. |
| Workers & lodging | Workers mine/gather/craft at nodes on timers — passive material and silver income. |
| Nodes & node connections | Unlock resources, worker jobs, and trade-route profit; connected networks matter. |
| Housing / town buildings | Processing/cooking/storage/worker lodging, function per town. |

## The economy layer

| System | What it does |
|---|---|
| Central Market | Server-wide auction house with price bands and taxes; the hub where life-skill output becomes silver. |
| Value Pack & buffs | Subscription-style buffs affecting market tax, weight, inventory — **verify current terms.** |
| Imperial delivery | Sell cooking/alchemy/other craft output to NPCs for guaranteed silver on a daily cap. |
| Silver sinks | Enhancement, accessories, CP-adjacent costs — where the money goes. |

## What couples it all together

- **Silver is the universal currency** — every engine feeds it and the combat engine burns it.
- **Life skills fund gear.** For a gear-poor, life-skill-rich account, the empire + cooking/processing
  are the intended way to bankroll the enhancement grind without deep combat farming.
- **Mastery is the life-skill multiplier** — Manos gear + mastery accessories raise yields across
  gathering/processing/cooking/etc., so investing in mastery pays into every life-skill loop at once.
- **CP and Energy are shared budgets** — spent across nodes, workers, and gathering; how you allocate
  them shapes total passive income.
