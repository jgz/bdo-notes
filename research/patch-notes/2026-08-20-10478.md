New Additions and Improvements

Today's update includes PvE balance adjustments for some classes and PvP balance adjustments for Agent.
As with the previous PvE balance update, these adjustments aim to narrow the damage gap between classes while taking into account each class's survivability, stability, and the fatigue and difficulty of combat. We also have plans for further balance adjustments to some classes.
With Inner Edania now introducing new monster zones and items, we will continue to make careful adjustments to provide Adventurers with a better PvE experience. Thank you.

Agent

First, to further reinforce Agent's assassin identity, the PvP damage of skills that embody this concept has been increased. The Defensive Effects and CC Effects of certain skills have also been adjusted so that Agent can create assassination opportunities by attempting CC from range.
Alongside this, considering Agent's role in PvP, we have improved his somewhat lacking survivability.

However, the Game Tips section from the previous Progression Pass remains available.

War Proposal
Alongside the Guild Fame system update, the guild war declaration system has also changed, allowing guilds to declare war without the opposing guild's consent by spending Guild Fame.
However, even in cases where both guilds wanted a war, only the guild that declared it first had to spend Guild Fame.
To address this, the War Proposal function has been added, allowing both guilds to enter a war without spending Guild Fame if they both agree.
Added a War Proposal button next to the existing War Declaration button in the War Declaration UI.

Using this button to propose war to another guild does not decrease Guild Fame.
The war begins only if the target guild accepts it, and since both sides agreed to it, neither guild loses Guild Fame.
A war started by accepting a proposal lasts for 6 hours.
War started by accepting a War Proposal has no wait time after it ends and another proposal can be sent immediately after.

Added an option to always reject war proposals with the addition of the War Proposal function.

Menu (ESC) - Settings - Misc. - Reject Guild War Proposals

Fairy Appearance and Skill Selection
Black Desert features fairies with a variety of special appearances beyond the default.
However, Adventurers could not freely choose an appearance for their fairy without giving up their preferred inherent skill, as each appearance came with a different skill effect.
With this update, you can now freely choose your fairy's appearance and skill separately.
The fairy's appearance and skill can now be selected separately.
Select the desired appearance from the list, then choose the skill to use at the bottom to apply it.
Owned appearances are shown in the list and appearances not owned can be purchased via the Purchase button at the bottom.

However, limited appearances whose sale period has ended will not be shown in the list.
Only skills from appearances you currently own can be selected.

Cup Extraction Change
Extracting a Cup from a reformed accessory previously required Refined Essence of Emotions. This system was originally designed to provide additional uses for various items obtained from sources such as Elvia monster zones.
Now that numerous updates have brought us to Inner Edania, we determined that this purpose has diminished and that the system mainly creates inconvenience when extracting Cups from accessories. As such, we have made Cup extraction from accessories easier and more convenient.

Guild Strategy Mapping
The Guild Strategy Mapping feature has been added to help guild members share strategies and coordinate more effectively.
You can now use this feature to mark paths directly on the World Map and set each mapping's name, color, and shape. A variety of other functions have been added to Guild Strategy Mapping as well. We hope this addresses some of the difficulties Adventurers have faced when planning strategies for content such as Node Wars and Conquest Wars.
We will continue to improve features that help guild members share information and work together more efficiently. Thank you.
Added the Map Guild Strategy feature to the World Map.

A Map Guild Strategy button has been added to the top of the World Map (M). Press it to open the window.

You can assign a hotkey for Map Guild Strategy via World Map Hotkey Settings at the bottom right of the World Map (M).
The Map Guild Strategy UI button is displayed only when you have joined a guild.
Using Map Guild Strategy requires the Map Guild Strategy authority. Guild members without authority can only view existing mappings.

Created mappings can be viewed on both the World Map (M) and minimap.
The functions of each button in Map Guild Strategy are as follows:

① Create Mapping: Creates a mapping.
- You can create up to 10 mappings, and each mapping can contain up to 10 points.

② Add Note: Sets a name for the mapping.

③ Settings: Sets the mapping's shape and color.

④ Delete: Deletes the mapping.

* To edit a mapping, press DELETE to delete points in reverse order, starting with the most recently created point.
* To delete a specific point, hover the mouse cursor over it and press DELETE.
* Guild members with authority can also create a mapping by holding CTRL and clicking the mouse wheel without selecting Create Mapping.
