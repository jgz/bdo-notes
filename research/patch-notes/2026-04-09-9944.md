Red Battlefield Improvements
As part of our continued efforts to balance the rewards from different content so you can earn Silver and enjoy PvP at the same time, Red Battlefield has been improved. We increased the number of Battlefield Tokens obtained on victory and changed the Exchange List to be more appealing. However, we have designed these rewards to only be given to Adventurers who truly enjoy Red Battlefield by adopting the auto-matchmaking system from the Arena of Solare and set minimum requirements to obtain Battlefield Tokens in order to minimize griefing. Please note that this minimum requirement is not based on your strengths, but on how passionately you have been enjoying Red Battlefield.
Changed the entry method for the Red Battlefield.

Instead of manually selecting a room, you will now be automatically matched by pressing the Find Match button.
Once 40 or more Adventurers apply, a match will be found and an Accept button will appear.
Upon accepting, you will automatically enter the Red Battlefield.
Changed the Red Battlefield location from Valencia City to Castle Ruins.

Added minimum requirements to receive rewards. You must meet at least one of the following to earn Red Battlefield rewards:

Defeat enemy Adventurer 3 times
15 deaths
Deal 200,000 damage
Take 100,000 damage
If 21 or more Adventurers leave within 2 minutes of entering the Red Battlefield, the match will be canceled and all Adventurers will exit.

Adjusted the number of Battlefield Tokens obtained based on Red Battlefield results.

Storage Memo and World Map Storage UI
As the world of Black Desert expanded, so have your needs for storage. Because of this, we've received feedback from Adventurers that they found it difficult to keep track of where all the items are among large amounts of items they keep in their expanded storage. To address this, we have added a Storage Note function that allows you to note down the purpose of each storage and easily identify information you need.
Moreover, we have improved the Storage UI on the World Map (M) into a list so it can be viewed at once, much like NPC Storages. We hope these changes help make your adventures more convenient.
Added a Memo function to Storage.

Purpose and details of storage in each town can now be recorded.

The Storage UI in the World Map (M) has been changed to a list, where other town's storages and memos can be seen.
