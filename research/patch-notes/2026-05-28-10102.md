New Additions and Improvements
All Classes
Greetings, Adventurers,
The PvP balance adjustments we have been implementing every week will conclude with this update.
As we enter the latter half of the Arena of Solare regular season, we aim to maintain a stable balance for the time being rather than introducing drastic changes to your combat experience. Of course, we will continue to monitor and review PvP balance, carefully addressing any areas that require adjustments in the future.
Additionally, our next character balance adjustments will focus on PvE. We plan to share details regarding these PvE balance adjustments sequentially, starting this June.
We will continue to monitor each class's performance to ensure their unique identity and role across various types of content.
Thank you.

Kunoichi
Kunoichi Awakening is a melee DPS and an assassin. However, because she could easily maintain Super Armor to deal damage safely despite her assassination potential, we have adjusted her skill combos to leave appropriate openings, bringing her more in line with her intended role.
We have also adjusted Penumbral Blade, an exceptionally fast and powerful skill, to give opponents a fair chance to react by increasing its wind-up time and removing its CC effects.

Central Market Max Price Adjustments
We have adjusted the Central Market max prices for certain items for the same reason as the April official server update: to address items that are rarely traded because their Central Market prices are too low compared to the cost of obtaining or crafting them.
For instance, items like Energy Potions, which have seen increased demand due to recent Gathering updates, and the rare Triple-Float Fishing Rod, which has limited acquisition sources, were rarely traded because their market prices did not properly reflect these circumstances.
We hope this update helps sellers earn more and helps buyers more easily purchase the items they need for more enjoyable adventures.

Improved Overload Conditions for Big Ships
Improved the overload condition so that all big ships only become overloaded when they exceed their max weight limit.

As a result, ships will no longer become overloaded when their current load matches their max weight limit, and sailing skills can be used normally.

UI
Item Drop Cheer Animation Removal
Previously, when you obtained a valuable item during your adventure, your character would cheer. While this clearly indicated that you had obtained a valuable item, the animation could put players in danger when it triggered unexpectedly in the heat of battle.
To keep the notification while preventing this risk, we have removed the cheering animation and added a UI notification instead.
Improved the system so that a notification is displayed instead of the character performing a cheer animation when obtaining high-grade loot during PvE.

However, characters will still perform the cheer animation as before when obtaining high-grade items through other means, such as opening a box.
The notification UI position can be adjusted through Edit UI.

Added reward details to the War of the Roses application UI.

Improved Find My Item to open when pressing CTRL+F even while the Quests UI is open.

System
Arena of Solare Penalty
Thank you for your continued interest and support for the ongoing Arena of Solare regular season. Today, we would like to introduce a new system being added to Arena of Solare.

Arena of Solare is 3v3 team-based PvP content. Naturally, each team member's performance is a crucial factor directly tied to victory.

Recently, we have received many reports of Adventurers entering Arena of Solare and remaining idle, disrupting the gameplay experience of other Adventurers. In this update, we are introducing a penalty system for such idle, or ""AFK,"" behavior.

Going forward, inactivity in Arena of Solare will result in penalties based on specific criteria. The severity of these penalties will increase with repeated offenses, up to a 30-day matchmaking restriction.

However, because the system applies the criteria uniformly when determining whether certain behavior is problematic, we have set the criteria very carefully to reduce the chance of Adventurers being penalized despite making a genuine effort to play.

This system is part of our efforts to improve the overall Arena of Solare experience for Adventurers. With this goal in mind, we will continue seeking better ways to improve the system.

We hope you continue to enjoy the remainder of the Arena of Solare regular season. Thank you.
Added a penalty system for players who remain idle or perform equivalent behaviors during Arena of Solare ranked matches.

Penalties increase with repeated offenses, up to a maximum 30-day matchmaking restriction for Arena of Solare.
Penalized Adventurer will receive a deserter penalty (-35 points) and will not receive any victory or defeat rewards.
A penalty notification will be displayed to the penalized Adventurer once the match ends.
Attempting to matchmake while a penalty is applied will display a notification on matchmaking restriction.
