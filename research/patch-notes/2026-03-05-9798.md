Inland Fishing Profit Improvements
Rumors are spreading that rare fish have formed a new ecosystem in the Duvencrune inland and coastal waters near Altinova.

White Crucian Carp have started appearing along the shore next to the Duvencrune Hot Spring.
Hammer Mackerel, Yellow Corvina, and Blue Bat Star have also started to appear along the Altinova coast.
Increased the spawn rate of White Crucian Carp by about 4.9 times in the existing fishing spots near Duvencrune.

Farming Byproduct Adjustments
In Dec 2025, the Contribution Point byproducts for most Life skills were rebalanced. For Farming, the quantity of Blush Leaves obtained from breeding "magical crops" was significantly higher than other Life Skill content. As such, we've adjusted these values to maintain overall balance.
Additionally, we increased the drop chance and reduced the quantity variance of Blush Leaves for crops leading up to the magical grade tier for a more stable and consistent yield.
Finally, since it was previously difficult to gain Farming EXP through harvesting, we have increased the EXP gained from harvesting.
Adjusted the amount and probability of obtaining Pink Leaf through Plant Breeding as follows:

General and Special crops refer to those that take up 1 slot in a garden.
Magical crops refer to those that take up 5 slots in a garden.
Type	Qty	Drop Rate
General	4-10 → 1-3	5x higher
High-quality	4-10 → 2-4	5x higher
Special	4-10 → 3-4	5x higher
Magical	20-50 → 20-22	No change

Increased the Farming EXP gained from harvesting.

Adjusted harvesting to grant about 80 percent of the Farming EXP earned from Plant Breeding.

Monster
Black Shrine - Donghae (Solo) - Oduksini Calamity 8 to 10
Oduksini, having regained its power within the deep darkness, has been added to the Black Shrine at Calamity 8, 9, and 10.
This new Oduksini will overwhelm the battlefield with greater ferocity than before. If you fail to properly guide the energy emitted during combat, the tide of battle can turn against you in an instant. As the final boss of Donghae Province, Oduksini has a higher min AP requirement than the Apex Changui. Consequently, the loot obtained upon a successful Boss Blitz has also been increased.
We look forward to seeing our Adventurers stand against the mightier Oduksini.
Added new difficulty levels, Calamity 8, 9, and 10, for the boss Oduksini in Black Shrine - Donghae (Solo).
You must meet the required stats below to take on each difficulty:
Boss Difficulty	Required Stat
Calamity 8	365+ Displayed AP
Calamity 9	375+ Displayed AP
Calamity 10	385+ Displayed AP
* For Calamity 8 or higher, stats from Light Orb (Sun, Moon, Earth Aura) are not applied. Only the stats from your character's equipped gear at entry will count.
* Weekly Boss Blitz attempts are shared with the current Black Shrine - Donghae (Solo).
