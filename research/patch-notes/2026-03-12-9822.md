Drakania
Drakania Awakening has been adjusted to balance the overpowered Dragonblood: Tip of the Scale and further define the roles of her Hexeblood and Dragonblood skills. Previously, Dragonblood: Tip of the Scale combined wide-range Knockdown with Drakania Awakening's mobility, which led it to function as an engage AoE CC skill that could also bypass opponents' Forward Guard. Accordingly, we've reduced the effectiveness of Dragonblood: Tip of the Scale. Dragonblood skills have been adjusted to maintain defensive effects and focus on patiently looking for openings. In contrast, Hexeblood skills have been adjusted to use quick actions to apply CC to a small number of enemies, then commit to fast, heavy damage in exchange for taking on greater risk.

War of the Roses
War of the Roses is a large-scale PvP mode where the leading guild's ability to command its faction plays a crucial role. However, practical issues, such as gathering 100 guild members at the same time or having insufficient guild/alliance participation, can make it difficult to apply.

To make this easier for the leading guild, we've improved the way members can be allocated. The leading guild will be able to recruit additional members from the third legion to fill in, and focus on their strategy instead of worrying about recruitment. However, please note that the additional recruitment will not be available if the number of applicants from the third legion is below 400.

We are also addressing the practical inconveniences you may have experienced while organizing platoons. With only the Family Name of the third legion available, the leading guild had to manually find out if the legion had enough experience in Node or Conquest Wars. Through this update, you will now see their affiliated guild in the platoon menu, allowing for more precise strategic planning.

Furthermore, the map has been restructured for a denser tactical element by reducing the number of Sanctums from 10 to 7 and placing them around the middle and lower parts of the map, instead of the distant outskirts that saw little action. With less time spent on unnecessary Sanctum defense, you will be able to focus your firepower on more focused and thrilling battles for key objectives.

Through these improvements, we hope to make War of the Roses more than a simple large-scale battle, into an even more interesting combination of strategy and combat.

UI
For seasoned Adventurers who travel across territories and servers to watch prices, what matters most is making a quick call at the best rate. The existing "Sell All" option is simple, but it did not leave much room to respond to different price trends for each item.

With this update, we have added the "Sell Same Type" function. Even if your Inventory (I) contains a mix of trade items, you can now choose exactly which goods to sell.

We hope this helps you fine-tune your Trading strategy: use the Quotation Board to sell favorable items first, and hold onto the rest until the timing is right.
Added the "Sell Same Type" function to the [Trade Manager] NPCs, allowing you to select and sell specific types of trade items from your inventory at once.

Select the Sell Same Type button for a specific item on the Quotation Board to sell all items of that type.
After a Bargain Success, an effect will highlight the individual sell button to make it easier to find.
You can now access various World Map (M) functions more conveniently via hotkeys. Whether you are setting an auto-run loop for Training, checking your horses at the Stable, or viewing the Barter UI while sailing, you can assign hotkeys to the functions you need and use them without clicking through menus. Use the "World Map Hotkey Settings" menu to build your own customized list and streamline your adventure.
Pressing CTRL after opening the World Map (M) will now show hotkey tooltips.

Press the displayed key along with CTRL or CTRL+SHIFT to open the corresponding menu.
Change the keys via the Cogwheel button (World Map Hotkey Settings) at the bottom right of the World Map.

Note: CTRL and CTRL+SHIFT combinations are fixed and cannot be changed.
