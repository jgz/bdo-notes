New Additions and Improvements

As announced in the update from the last week of May, class PvE balance adjustments are underway.
As previously explained, with the Arena of Solare entering the latter half of its season, we will focus PvP-related updates on maintaining the current flow as much as possible. However, please note that we may still adjust skill combos or cooldowns to make necessary improvements with minimal impact on PvP.
To start, we have made PvE balance adjustments for several classes today. This update buffs classes with lower PvE damage to narrow the overall damage gap. We are also preparing extra balance adjustments to address class survivability, stability, PvE difficulty, and play fatigue, which we will introduce as soon as they are ready.
Additionally, we are reviewing classes that deal sufficient damage but have structural issues unrelated to damage output, such as skill combo flow. Since resolving these issues will inevitably affect PvP balance, these class updates will be released when the PvP season is not in progress.
We will do our best to provide a more improved PvE experience. Thank you.
