Guild Fame
Guilds are an integral part of Black Desert, bringing Adventurers together to share adventures, create stories, and make lasting memories.

The new Guild Fame system turns your individual activities into your guild's growth, which in turn provides benefits for you. Guild members can earn Guild Fame through various activities and unlock guild-wide benefits based on their Guild Fame. Guild Fame can also be spent to activate special effects. By encouraging guilds to earn and replenish the Guild Fame they spend, we hope to make guild activities more engaging.

We hope you continue to enjoy adventuring with your guild and create lasting memories with the Guild Fame system.
* Added the Guild Fame system, which allows guilds to earn Fame through various activities and unlock benefits for all guild members based on the total earned.

Guild War
The guild war declaration system has been revamped.
Guilds can now spend 400,000 Guild Fame to declare war without the opposing guild's consent. However, each war will last only 6 hours, and once it ends, the same guild cannot be targeted again for 48 hours.

Previously, guilds often found themselves subjected to unwanted war declarations. More recently, however, many guilds have expressed frustration over being unable to declare war even when necessary.
With the Guild Fame update, we have revised the guild war system to better balance these concerns.
Guild war can be declared with Guild Fame.
Declaring a war consumes 400,000 Guild Fame.

At least 10 guild members must be online to declare a war. However, you can respond to a guild war declared against your guild without requirements.
Guild war can be declared regardless of the target guild's size or number of members.
Guild war can be declared on up to 6 guilds at once.
Declaring war on guilds with negative Guild Fame does not consume Guild Fame.
Guild war cannot be declared when Guild Fame is below -1,600,000.
A war declaration lasts for 6 hours.
However, if the opposing guild responds to the declaration, the war lasts 7 days.
You cannot declare war on the same guild again for 48 hours.
At least 7 days must pass after creating a guild to declare war.
Guild war starts 5 min after declaration.
After declaring war, the changed Guild Fame can be seen in the Guild (G) UI.
The war declaration history can be checked via Guild (G) - Guild History.

UI
Marni's Combat Analyzer
Marni's Combat Analyzer has been added, allowing you to review the flow and results of your combat.
You can now easily view damage dealt, damage taken, DPS (Damage per Second), combat duration, and more. The individual combat analysis provides a more detailed breakdown of your combat records from multiple perspectives.
However, please note that this feature is available only in select content to help ensure server stability.
We hope Marni's Combat Analyzer serves as a useful tool for testing different setups and skill combos while objectively tracking your progression.
Added Marni's Combat Analyzer.
Go to Menu (ESC) - Character (F2) - Marni's Combat Analyzer to access the feature.
If the "Hide UI when Struck" option is enabled in Settings, the Combat Analyzer UI will be hidden when hit, but recording will continue.
The widget will remain visible even when "Hide UI when Struck" is enabled.

① Main Window
- Displays DMG Dealt, DMG Taken, DPS (Damage per Second), and Combat Duration.
- Exceeding the maximum recording time of 1 hour and 10 minutes requires a reset before recording again.
- Press the Sticker UI button to pop the window outside the game screen.
- Toggle the widget on or off via the Open/Close Widget button.
* If "Hide UI when Struck" is enabled, the Marni's Combat Analyzer UI will disappear upon being hit, but the widget remains on screen.

② Individual Combat Analysis - Tabs and Filters
- Access Individual Combat Analysis for detailed logs.
- Switch between tabs to view DMG Dealt, DMG Taken, and CC records. Filter records by skill, monster, or character name.
- If multiple targets share the same name, a number is appended to each target name for distinction.
- Press the "All Characters" button, choose a target, and input a number in the box to view data for that specific target.
- Press the "All Skills" button to view logs by skill.

③ Individual Combat Analysis - Record List
- Displays Time, Detailed Information, and Damage.
- Time: Displays the recorded time.
- Detailed Information: Displays whether damage was dealt or taken, alongside the skill name.
- Damage: Displays the damage dealt/taken, CC type, and whether it was a Critical Hit.

④ Start, Stop, and Reset Recording
- Press the Start button to begin recording, or press it again to pause recording.
- Start and Reset buttons can be assigned to Quick Hotkeys.
- Pressing the Reset button deletes all recorded data.
