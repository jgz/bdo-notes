Yar! Matchmaking
Added matchmaking to the Yar! minigame.

The Yar! minigame has been improved to allow playing with Adventurers from other servers
Example: An adventurer on Calpheon4 can match with an Adventurer on Serendia2.
Select a match from the existing Yar! invite UI to play.
The match difficulty is 5 stars (★) with 4 card draws.
Yar! match history can be checked immediately.
Matches are made regardless of the match history.

Fixed the issue where the difficulty would not be set when selecting the star-shaped area on the Yar! invite UI.

UI

The Monster Zone Info UI is widely used by Adventurers to get useful information about various monster zones without having to visit them directly. While this UI serves many purposes, it is most frequently used to find monster zones suited to your current stats. With this update, we have added a feature that displays monster zones suited to your current total AP, as well as zones worth challenging, with just a single click. We hope this makes your adventures more convenient.
Added the Recommended Zone function to the Monster Zone Info UI.

Recommends monster zones within ±50 of your character's current total AP.
The Recommended Zone function filters the list to show only monster zones that meet the conditions, making it easier to find zones that match your AP.

Please note that recommendations are based on total AP, and DP is not factored into the recommendations.
Recommended Total AP is calculated by adding Extra AP Against Monsters and Extra AP Against Species for each zone, regardless of your Stat Display Setting.
