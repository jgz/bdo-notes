Barter Improvements
Today, we are excited to share the Barter update first announced at the Calpheon Ball.

Barter is content where Adventurers generate revenue by trading goods for higher-level, more valuable items. Generally, this involves sailing between islands to complete trades and returning to port once the weight limit is reached.

In this update, we have added Level 6 and Level 7 trade routes connecting the major ports of each continent. While these new routes require traveling between distant continents, they are easier to manage and offer significantly higher revenue than before. To preserve the core experience of planning and navigating your own trade routes, barter goods can no longer be moved via Transport.

We have also addressed several quality-of-life issues encountered during Barter. We hope you enjoy these improvements to the trade experience.

Altar of Blood Revamp
As announced during the last Calpheon Ball, we have revamped the Altar of Blood, a mode where three Adventurers come together in a party to defend the relic.

With the current maximum AP at 395, we have updated the mode up to the 21st Illusion, and higher Illusions will be added as the maximum available AP increases. Additionally, because there is no AP limit in the Altar of Blood, the higher your AP, the faster you will be able to defeat monsters.

Meanwhile, the required AP for matchmaking into the 21st Illusion is currently set to 400, which cannot yet be reached. This was intended to provide a challenge for Adventurers who wish to overcome the limits of their gear through teamwork, and you will still be able to enter the Illusion by queueing as a pre-made party. Additionally, we are currently preparing a new difficulty for Adventurers who have cleared all levels, which will include new challenge elements.

We hope this unforgiving defense against waves of enemies provides a new challenge and excitement for you.
The abyssal rift has reawakened. The Altar of Blood returns, calling for brave Adventurers to protect the sanctum.

You can enter the Altar of Blood via solo matching or as a 3-player party. Three Adventurers must cooperate to protect the relic from waves of monsters or defeat powerful bosses.
The Altar of Blood consists of 21 stages. You must clear the previous stage to progress to the next.

Higher difficulty levels may be added in the future.
When entering Altar of Blood as a party, the highest stage your party can challenge is based on the lowest clear record among all party members.

For example, if two members have cleared up to Stage 5 and one has cleared up to Stage 3, the party can only enter up to Stage 4 (the next challenge for the Stage 3 member).
If you exhaust all 5 attempts provided upon matching, your party will be automatically moved out of the Altar of Blood.

There is no weekly limit on entries. You can re-apply for matching and enter again at any time.
In addition to weekly rewards, you can obtain a first-time clear reward once per Family for each stage.
Clear records do not reset. You can resume your challenge from the highest stage you have previously reached.

* Altar of Blood Guide
The Altar of Blood is divided into normal stages and boss stages.

In normal stages, your main purpose is to defeat monsters rushing toward the central relic.
If monsters reach the relic, its HP will decrease. If the relic's HP reaches 0%, the stage will fail.

In boss stages, a specific boss appears. Your goal is to defeat the boss while completing specific mechanics.
Bosses enter berserk form 6 minutes after appearing, significantly increasing their AP and DP.
If the boss is not defeated within 8 minutes of appearing, the stage will fail.

The Altar of Blood consists of 21 stages, from The 1st Illusion to The 21st Illusion.

For solo matching, you must meet the minimum AP and DP requirements for each stage.
If matching as a 3-player party, you can enter even if you do not meet the minimum requirements.

How to participate in the Altar of Blood:

Go to Menu (ESC) - War (F7) - Find Match in the Altar of Blood menu.

If you apply as a 3-player party, you will enter with your party members. If you apply solo, you will be matched with other Adventurers.
The Party Leader may change based on the order of entry.
* Altar of Blood Rules
Upon entry, the Party Leader selects the stage.
Once the Party Leader selects a stage and presses Start Game, that stage begins immediately.
Resurrection is unavailable if you die (Witch/Wizard Resurrection skills and Medical Kits cannot be used).
All characters resurrect and recover HP upon completing or failing a stage.
If all three party members die, the stage fails. All monsters will disappear, and members will revive with full HP. The stage selection UI will then appear.
Campsites, Maid/Butler, World Map, Quests UI, and Minimap UI are unavailable.
Combat and Skill EXP cannot be obtained from defeating monsters.
Item Drop Rate and Item Drop Amount effects do not apply.
If combat does not start or there is no progression for 10 min after entry, you will be automatically removed from the altar.

Clearing stages in the Altar of Blood grants weekly rewards and first-time clear rewards.

Weekly Rewards
Based on your weekly records, rewards are sent to the Black Spirit's Safe every Sunday at 00:00 (Once a week per Family).
Rewards are available for each stage, but you will only receive the reward for the highest stage cleared during that week.

Example: Even if you clear Stages 10, 15, and 20 in the same week, rewards do not stack. You will only receive the Stage 20 weekly reward.
First Clear Rewards
When you clear each stage for the first time, rewards are automatically sent to Black Spirit's Safe (B).
 Higher stages offer better rewards, with more item types, larger quantities, and higher chances.

Treasure Material Appraisal Removal
Black Desert has 3 types of treasure items that could be appraised to find out which treasure they were materials for, offering the excitement of uncovering the mystery behind each one.

However, as information such as which monsters drop them became widely known, Adventurers could often tell what item they had obtained based on the system message or the location where it dropped. As a result, the original thrill and excitement of appraising these items diminished.

Therefore, we have removed the appraisal process for treasure materials and changed it so the item type is revealed immediately, making it easier to identify which material was obtained.
Improved treasure materials so they can now be identified without appraisal.

This change applies to the Archaeologist's Map, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass, and the Rich Merchant's Ring.
As materials can now be identified without appraisal, the appraisal dialogue option has been removed from the following NPCs:

Caphras's Journal (Archaeologist's Map)
Ain Greid (Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass)
Shakatu (Rich Merchant's Ring)
The quest summary for "Lafi Bedmountain's Telescope Parts" has been partially updated.
You can now obtain knowledge for treasure materials by showing the Archaeologist's Map, Lafi Bedmountain's Upgraded Compass, Rich Merchant's Ring, or their respective materials to Lavala at Crow's Nest.

Note: Knowledge can still be obtained through the existing NPCs (Caphras's Journal, Ain Greid, and Shakatu).

World Map Search Function Improvement
The World Map is one of the most frequently used displays for checking routes, managing nodes, and finding NPCs. However, many Adventurers felt that the search function was too limited and that useful info was scattered across different UIs, making it hard to find a destination quickly.

In this update, we have redesigned the search bar to support unified search, allowing you to find various information in one place. We will continue to improve the World Map to ensure it serves as a convenient tool for exploration, Life Skills, and progress.

Improved the World Map's search function to be more convenient.

Changed the design of the search UI and improved it to allow integrated search for various information in a single search bar.
Search for the following items in the UI:
- Node Product
- Node Name
- NPC Name
- Key functions such as Storage and Repair
Production Node Filter from the Production Node Information has been combined into the button on the right side of the search bar.
The function to navigate to a selected City or Town in the previous search list was changed to the Territory icon button at the top of the search UI, and can be used in the same way as before.
The search also displays results even if the geography or NPC knowledge is not acquired, and selecting the result moves the camera to that location.

Boss Info Improvement
Many Adventurers participate in boss subjugation, including World Bosses, Field Bosses, Dark Rifts, and Hunting Bosses. Previously, this information was scattered, making it difficult to see which content was available or where to go. To improve this, we have introduced a unified Boss Info UI.

Through this new UI, you can check the time remaining until a boss appears and view the remaining HP after they have spawned. Selecting a boss from the list will also provide navigation to their location. You can access this via the Menu or by pressing the Dark Rift icon in the bottom right. We will continue to improve accessibility so you can easily jump into the content you enjoy.

Added a Boss Info UI so that boss information can be checked more easily.

Open the UI via Menu (ESC) -> Adventure, or by pressing the Dark Rift button at the bottom right.

Pressing a boss icon on the World Map (M) also displays the Boss Info.
The Boss Info UI combines the Dark Rift and Boss Notification functions into one.

Black Shrine Reward Distribution Improvement
The way Black Shrine rewards are distributed has been improved. Previously, any unclaimed Black Shrine Boss Blitz rewards could not be obtained the following week. To improve this, Boss Blitz rewards will now be automatically sent to Black Spirit's Safe at a designated time, and your Boss Blitz history can be found in the Reward Log within Black Spirit's Safe.

Red Battlefield Matchmaking Improvements
Improved the conditions for starting and continuing Red Battlefield matches, and added penalties for not accepting matches.

If 30 or more Adventurers accept a match, only those who accepted will enter, and those who did not will be excluded from the match.
A penalty preventing match requests will be applied if you repeatedly do not accept after the match Accept/Decline UI appears.
