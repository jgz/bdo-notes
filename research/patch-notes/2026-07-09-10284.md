New Additions and Improvements

Thank you very much for your interest in the PvE balance adjustments over the past few weeks.
Today's update marks the end of this round of PvE balance adjustments. Moving forward, we will closely monitor the changes we have made and take some time to prepare for further improvements.
We fully understand the importance of class balance, as it directly impacts your gameplay experience. That is why we are constantly exploring better approaches.
During this break from balance updates, we will thoroughly review the process and results of the recent adjustments to identify areas for improvement. We will do our best to deliver balance updates that better meet your expectations in the future.
Thank you once again to all our Adventurers for your continued feedback.

With the damage and usability of Prime: Forward Slash and Absolute: Ground Slash improved, incorporating these two skills into your combos will now result in smoother combos and more consistent damage.

Black Shrine - Donghae (Solo): Songakshi Calamity 8-10
Songakshi, the tormented soul who struck fear into the hearts of the young men of the Land of the Morning Light, returns in an even more powerful form.
With the Calamity 8, 9, and 10 difficulties added in this update, we have introduced brand-new mechanics to deliver a fresh challenge. The overall difficulty of this Boss Blitz is similar to the recently added Duoksini and Oduksini.
We hope your courage and wisdom will allow you to defeat Songakshi, who radiates a chilling aura, and protect the peace of the Land of the Morning Light.
Added Calamity 8, 9, and 10 difficulties for the Black Shrine - Donghae (Solo) boss, Songakshi.
Added the following stat requirements to challenge each boss difficulty.
Boss Difficulty	Required Stats

Mount
Guild Mounts play a vital role in guild-level PvP content, such as Node/Conquest Wars, and the Blue Battlefield.
Due to their importance, only guild members with a specific permission are allowed to manage them. However, Mount Transport was available regardless of this permission, so we have corrected this.
Changed the Mount Transport function for guild mounts to only be accessible by guild members with Manage Guild Mounts authority.

UI
Character Note on the Exit Game UI
We have improved the usability of the Character Note feature.
Previously, notes saved in the Family Character List UI on the World Map could only be viewed within that list. With this update, notes written in the Family Character List will now also be displayed on both the Character Selection screen and the Exit Game UI.
We expect this change to help you quickly check essential information and keep track of each character's status or plans while managing multiple characters in your Family.

Improved the character notes written in the View Family Characters UI of the World Map to display above the character names on the Exit Game and Character Selection UI.

However, writing and editing notes must still be done via World Map (M) → View Family Characters.
Notes written on the World Map are saved locally on your PC (client) and will not appear when logging in from a different device.

Improved so a guide on mount Stamina is displayed for New Adventurers when they board a mount that is ready to level up and has less than 30 Stamina remaining.

This guide is displayed for the following mounts: horses, camels, elephants, and donkeys.

Changed the World Map (M) - View Family Characters UI so that selecting another character while viewing a character's inventory now displays the newly selected character's inventory.

Changed the position of the Storage button that appears at the top when opening the Storage in the World Map (M) to the far right.

Improved the Durability Warning UI to display based on the remaining expiration period when equipping a fishing rod that has an expiration date.

The warning UI will display if the expiration date is approaching, even if there is remaining Durability.
