HYPERBOOST
New and returning Adventurers may sometimes find themselves unsure of where or how to begin.

With Edania Part 2, a new region for your adventures, on the horizon, we felt it was time to revamp the progression system so Adventurers still learning the ropes of Black Desert can grow more quickly. We have streamlined unnecessarily complex steps and reorganized key progression milestones to provide a smoother path forward.

After graduating from Season and completing the Olvia Academy combat course, you will only need to accomplish a few additional objectives to obtain three Sovereign weapons, a full set of Slumbering Origin defense gear, and PEN (V) Kharazad accessories for every slot. To make further progression smoother, the amount of Agris Essence required for Slumbering Origin defense gear will be significantly reduced. Additional measures will also be introduced to assist with Kharazad accessory enhancement.

Hyperboost is not simply about providing the equipment you need. Its goal is to help Adventurers learning the ropes of Black Desert stay on track, experience essential content naturally, and continue their journey without feeling lost. We will continue improving the progression experience so Adventurers can enjoy a wide range of content without unnecessary pressure.

We will always be here to support you on your shining journey. Thank you.

World Map Stable Info Improvements

The previous stable info UI allowed you to view mounts stored in each region. However, it was difficult to quickly check detailed information on mounts owned by your characters or stored in Krogdalo's Sanctuary.
With this update, you can now view accurate information on all mounts in your Family at once. The Mount Information UI now displays mounts owned by your characters, mounts checked in at stables, and mounts stored in Krogdalo's Sanctuary, along with their detailed information.
We hope this update makes it easier for Adventurers to manage their mounts, their essential companions on every adventure.
