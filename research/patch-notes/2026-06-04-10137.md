Crop Growth Time Changes
Crop growth times have been significantly increased, and yields from harvesting or breeding have been increased accordingly.
Similar to the previous update that extended fish storage periods, this change aims to reduce the pressure of having to check on your crops at set intervals. Additionally, growth times, which previously varied by crop, have now been standardized.
Changed the growth time required for crops to be 20 to 22 hours depending on the temperature.

Klau's Old Moon Seed Pouch Quality-of-Life Update
In the May 29 (Fri) Global Lab update, we announced plans to allow planting seeds directly from Klau's Old Moon Seed Pouch in your garden.
This feature was developed alongside the crop growth time changes to improve Adventurers' quality of life.

During final preparations for the live servers, we identified areas that needed more improvements.
As a result, the planned changes to where seeds obtained through Plant Breeding are stored, as well as the planned icon improvements, have also been delayed.
We will update you further on these features through the Global Lab or the live server patch notes.

We apologize for delivering this disappointing news to those who were eagerly anticipating these updates.
We were just as excited to bring you these quality-of-life improvements.
We will continue to look for ways to make farming more enjoyable. T

Reduced Contribution Costs for Residence and Node Investments
To help Adventurers utilize more nodes, the Contribution Points (CP) required to rent residences and invest in certain nodes have been reduced.
We considered increasing CP acquisition or reducing the EXP required at higher CP levels. However, we decided that reducing existing CP costs would be more convenient and less burdensome for Adventurers at this time.
We hope you will invest the extra Contribution Points freed up by today's update into more appealing nodes and discover new enjoyment in your adventures.

Production Node Additions and Changes
With this update, new Production Nodes have been added, and the items produced at certain existing nodes have been changed.
We previously identified several Production Nodes that offered relatively low utility or appeal for their required Contribution Points. To make these nodes more practical, we have improved their production items, expanding the options available to Adventurers.
We hope these changes allow you to manage your Production Nodes more flexibly according to your needs and secure more valuable resources than before.

Duoksini at these new difficulty levels threatens Adventurers with faster and more powerful attacks than in Calamity 7 and below. You must respond swiftly to his onslaught to seize your opportunity to counterattack.

Black Shrine: Donghae (Solo) - 6th Light Orb
With today's addition of Duoksini Calamity 8, 9, and 10, most Black Shrine - Donghae (Solo) bosses now have Calamity 8 to 10 difficulties available.
As many Adventurers are now challenging Calamity 8 through 10, the lower difficulties (Calamity 1 to 7) are primarily played by Adventurers who are still getting used to Black Desert.
Therefore, a 6th Light Orb has been added to ease the difficulty of Calamity 1 through 7 and help Adventurers complete these Boss Blitzes more easily.
If you found the Black Shrine difficult or still feel unfamiliar with it, equip the 6th Light Orb and take on the challenge.
Added the 6th Light Orb, which can be used in Black Shrine.

You can accept the new quest "[LoML] Talekkebi's Trials - Calamity 6" from the Black Spirit if you have completed all quests in the Tale of Koo Mihyun and possess the Tale of Koo Mihyun knowledge.
