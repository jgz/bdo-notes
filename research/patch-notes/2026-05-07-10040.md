Greetings, Adventurers.

Here are the details regarding the latest update applied to Black Desert on May 7, 2026.

Changes and Fixes
Fixed the issue where the game client would occasionally crash during a Node War.
Increased the maximum number of simultaneous matches available for the Arena of Solare.

Fixed the issue where event items were obtained in greater quantities than intended in the Star's End and Jade Starlight Forest monster zones.

To ensure that no Adventurer is disadvantaged by this issue, any shortfall in event item acquisition from other monster zones will be compensated with additional items.

- Period: After the April 30, 2026 (Thu) maintenance - Before May 7, 2026 (Thu) maintenance
- Eligible Adventurers: Adventurers who obtained event items in monster zones other than Star's End and Jade Starlight Forest
- Based on verified records, additional event items ([Event] Mysterious Ore, [Event] Blissful Box) will be provided to match the acquisition quantity that was available in Star's End and Jade Starlight Forest.
- Please note specific distribution dates will be announced later.

We apologize for the inconvenience and we hope that you are enjoying your adventures in Black Desert!
Thank you.
