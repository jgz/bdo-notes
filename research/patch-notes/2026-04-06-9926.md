Q. What happens if I don’t complete all courses by graduation (Apr 16)?

Graduation and the start of Class 2 are both on Apr 16.

If you wish to learn more, you can re-enroll in Class 2 to continue studying at the Academy, so don’t worry!

But, your study progress will reset to 0% when you re-enroll. So if you aren’t too far from 100%, we do recommend trying to complete it before graduation!

Q. Can I still graduate if I haven’t completed all courses?

You must complete all courses to 100% to begin the graduation quest.

Q. Do I need to claim the Family rewards before graduation?

Unclaimed rewards will stay right at the Olvia Academy UI, so there’s no need to worry.

However, please note that the rewards section of the UI is only visible to students, so you will need to enroll in Class 2.

Q. Should I graduate as early as I can? What’s recommended?

If you wish to continue using the academy’s cooking/alchemy tools, and the Abyssal Echo, we recommend graduating closer to Apr 15.

If you wish to graduate and receive rewards from Class 1, you must complete the quests from all courses before Class 2 begins, as all courses will reset.

Q. What will happen to my Olvia Academy Coins?

Your owned coins as well as the exchange items in the Manage Currency UI will be maintained. If you need more coins, you can enroll in Class 2 and obtain more through courses and Weekly Quests.

Q. What happens if I don’t graduate?

All courses and quests in progress will reset for all Adventurers including non-graduates during the Apr 16 (Thu) maintenance.

If you wish to re-enroll, find NPC Emma Bartali in the Administration Office to accept “[Olvia Academy] Your First Steps In”.

Q. What are the graduation rewards?

Graduates will receive Olvia Academy Coins x300, as well as the Olvia Academy Graduate title and knowledge.

Q. What happens to the Academy Pass Rewards I didn’t claim?

Unclaimed Academy Pass Rewards will be automatically sent to your Black Spirit’s Safe on the Apr 16 (Thu) maintenance.

📘 Class 1 Graduates

Q. I need more Olvia Academy Coin. Where can I get it?

You can obtain more Coins the same way after joining Class 2. Also remember that the exchange items will also remain!

Q. Can I re-enroll to Class 2?

Of course! Class 1 graduates can also find NPC Emma Bartali in the administration office and accept “[Olvia Academy] Your First Steps In” to re-enroll.

Q. Can I get the Family Rewards again if I re-enroll?

Rewards limited to once per Family, such as Family Reward – Combat, Family Reward – Life Skill, Class Number Title, Academia Outfit Box will not be available, but the academy cooking/alchemy tools issued to students will be available again.
📙 Adventurers yet to Enroll

Q. If the enrollment for Class 1 is open until Apr 9, should I enroll now?

If you have reached Lv. 60, we recommend enrolling in Class 1 to claim all Class 1 Olvia Academy Pass (available for Silver) rewards, then re-enroll in Class 2! [Pass Details]

Q. What are the enrollment rewards?

You will get Class Number Title and Academia Outfit Box for the first enrollment of your Family! Start your adventure with the academy’s various guides on combat and life skills!

Q. Which Class should I enroll in?

Enrollment and graduation rewards are identical except for the Class number in the Title, so enroll whenever you’d like. (Class 1 before Apr 9, Class 2 after Apr 16)

Enjoy your adventure at your leisure, then enroll based on your progress.
