Inner Edania
Agris, Markthanan, Elion, and Aal have at last revealed their presence in the deepest reaches of Edania. Now, you must prove your worth against their might and take another step along the path of destiny woven through Edania. Your choices and resolve will shape Edania's tomorrow, so make every preparation before taking on this challenge.
In Inner Edania, you will once again encounter the wide range of content introduced in the previous Edania region, now in entirely new forms, including Boss Subjugation, the Throne of Edana, main and sub-quests, Life content, and more. Along with this update and Hadum's arrival in September, we hope you enjoy the final chapter of Black Desert Act 1 to the fullest.
We await the courageous and wise Adventurers who will bring hope to Inner Edania, where an even deeper abyss awaits.
Thank you.

Inner Edania: Abyssal Veins
Edania is divided into the outer region, the domain of the Demonlords granted a share of the divine powers, and the inner region, where the gods exert their authority directly.
As such, entering the inner region from Outer Edania by normal means is extremely difficult.

Complete the quest below from the Black Spirit to enter Angavu Outpost in Inner Edania.
Along with this, 28 sub-quests have been added to Inner Edania.
Main questlines that continue the story of Edania will be added in a future update.

Throne of Edana
Defeat a boss of Aphrodon, Hermesia, Magaia, or Aresion, and you'll earn the right to become the Edana—the one who rules over that region.

Every Sunday, the chosen Edana must defend their throne against other Adventurers. Through battles with challengers, a new Edana is decided.
The Adventurer who ultimately claims the Edana’s throne will receive special privileges and rewards.
You cannot enter with any Dawn Crystal other than Dawn Crystal - All AP transfused.

This rule also applies to the Throne of Edana content in the Outer Edania.
The crown granted to the Adventurer who ascends the throne of Inner Edania is sent to the Angavu Storage.
How to Apply	Use the "Edana Rising Token" item, which can be obtained by completing the weekly boss subjugation quests.

Aphrodon (Agris): Edana Rising Token - Goldroot
Hermesia (Markthanan): Edana Rising Token - Ebonmere
Magaia (Elion): Edana Rising Token - Everlight
Aresion (Aal): Edana Rising Token - Crimsonflare
Entry Requirements	Aphrodon (Agris): 405+ AP / 485+ DP / At least "red-grade" gear equipped
Hermesia (Markthanan): 410+ AP / 490+ DP / At least "red-grade" gear equipped
Magaia (Elion): 415+ AP / 495+ DP / At least "red-grade" gear equipped
Aresion (Aal): 420+ AP / 505+ DP / At least "red-grade" gear equipped

* You must equip at least "red-grade" gear (weapons, defense gear, accessories), excluding Artifacts and Alchemy Stones, to enter.

 Added a combat mechanic using Black Spirit's Rage skills to boss battles in Inner Edania.

Monsters affected by this mechanic have a unique HP gauge.

Max Character Level
To make leveling a more meaningful part of progression, a max level will be introduced along with changes to the EXP structure. Going forward, Lv. 75 will become the max level, giving you a clear goal to work toward and a greater sense of accomplishment through various benefits earned at each level. To ensure this goal can be reached within a more realistic timeframe, we'll also completely revamp the EXP structure.
This update will be rolled out in two parts. As the first step, the max level has been set to Lv. 70 today. The Lv. 75 cap, EXP structure revamp, and level-based benefits are planned for release on the official servers on Oct 8.
We'll do our best to prepare these changes for you. Thank you.
Set the maximum reachable level for characters to Lv. 70.

Characters at max level will no longer gain Combat EXP.
The level cap is scheduled to expand to Lv. 75 in a future update (planned for Oct 8).
The overall EXP acquisition and level-up structure will be revamped alongside the Lv. 75 expansion.
Awarded the titles below during the Aug 13, 2026 (Thu) maintenance, in recognition of Adventurers who reached Lv. 70 or higher.

Until the Lv. 75 expansion update, all Adventurers who reach Lv. 70 can claim the Lv. 70 title from Challenge (Y).
Level achievement titles were also awarded for previous level milestones (e.g. A Lv. 72 Adventurer received the titles for reaching both Lv. 70 and Lv. 71).
However, as these titles honor Adventurers who reached Lv. 70 through long-term play and dedication, those who reached the required level using extra EXP from Secret Scrolls of Pure Equilibrium or Combat/Skill EXP Exchange Coupons are not eligible.

Once you reach the maximum level, you will no longer gain Combat EXP.
