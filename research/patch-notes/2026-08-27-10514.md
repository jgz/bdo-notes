New Additions and Improvements

As announced in a previous update, balance adjustments have been made to several classes.
Today's update addresses the gaps in monster zone entry timing caused by differences in AP (Total Stat) during PvE balancing, and also improves classes that were lacking in survivability or damage following changes to monster zone environments.
Further class changes for the current PvE and PvP environments will be announced through the Global Lab as soon as they are ready.

Grapple
Previously, when struck by a Grapple skill with a long animation, you could not leave that location until the opponent's skill animation had fully finished, even if you used Emergency Escape.
With this update, using Emergency Escape will immediately free you from the location, even during the Grapple animation. Accordingly, if the target uses Emergency Escape, the Grapple skill will immediately transition to its finishing motion and end.
In addition, to reduce cases where the target was not actually held despite a successful Grapple, we have added a slight delay before a Grapple connects into its follow-up attack motion. (The lead-in time for attempting a Grapple remains the same.) The duration of the Defensive Effects applied on a successful Grapple remains unchanged. We expect this adjustment to alleviate the issue.
We will continue addressing inconveniences so Adventurers can fully enjoy the PvP experience.
Fixed the issue where using Emergency Escape while grappled could fail to break the Grapple and return you to the grappled position.
Changed so that if the grappled target dies or uses Emergency Escape after a successful Grapple, the Grapple ends with a finishing motion instead of a Grapple failure motion.

This finishing motion does not apply the damage or CC effects normally applied upon a successful Grapple.
However, damage and effects from additional attack motions are still applied regardless of which motion applies the Grapple's CC effect. (e.g. Wukong's Gotcha!, Drakania's Impale)
Mitigated the issue where, after a successful Grapple, the grappled target could occasionally use skills or become ungrappled.

To address this, a brief delay has been added between a successful Grapple and the follow-up attack. However, the timing at which Defensive Effects apply upon a successful Grapple remains unchanged.

New Guild League Season
Thank you to all the guilds and Adventurers who have been waiting for the start of the Guild League regular season. In preparation for this season, we analyzed participation data from multiple angles and made several improvements based on our findings.
As Adventurers' gear specs and combat mastery have leveled up across the board and guild strategies have grown more sophisticated, the flow of battle has changed significantly. In one-sided matches, the leading guild would quickly reach 1,000 points and secure victory. In closely contested matches, however, both sides often struggled to reach 1,000 points, with the outcome decided only after the full 20-minute match duration.
To increase the overall pace of Guild League and deliver more action-packed matches, we have reduced the maximum match duration from 20 minutes to 12 minutes and changed the victory conditions from 1,000 points to 600 points. We have also shortened the match cycle from 30 minutes to 20 minutes, allowing guilds to enter their next match sooner. As match duration and participation cycles have been shortened, rewards have also been adjusted to reflect the new playtime.
We hope you enjoy even more exciting matches with your guild members in this faster-paced Guild League.

How to Participate

Guild Defender: Available when at least 7 guild members are logged in.
Defender Unit I: Available when at least 14 guild members are logged in, and after the Guild Defender group has applied for matchmaking.

Guild League Rules

Participants

7-10 members per group

Match Duration

12 min (Changed)

After matchmaking is complete, there is a 4 min waiting time upon entering Guild League. Guild members can enter during this period. (Changed)
There is a 1 min waiting time after the match ends, after which participants will be automatically removed from the battlefield.
Participants can freely enter and exit after the match begins.

Victory Conditions

Achieve 600 points, or have the higher score when the match ends. (Changed)

If the scores are tied at the end of the match, the guild that scored first wins.
Points

First Kill: 30 points (Changed)
Each Kill: 10 points

Revival

1 revival point exists at a designated location for each guild.

Characters revive automatically without a revive button upon death, and will automatically enter the combat zone after a set time within the veil.

There is no limit to the number of revivals.
Reviving grants Invincible and Damage Reduction effects for a set duration while out of combat.
Death penalties such as EXP loss and crystal destruction do not apply.

Penalties

Accumulating 3 or more penalties leads to a 1-week (168 hours) ban from Guild League matchmaking.

If fewer than 7 guild members enter during the waiting period after matchmaking, it will be treated as a defeat, the match will automatically end, and 1 penalty will be issued.
If the number of participating members drops below 4 during an ongoing match, it will be treated as a defeat, the match will automatically end, and 1 penalty will be issued.

Other Rules

If a participant exits during the Guild League, any member from the same guild can step in and re-enter.

However, recorded damage, CC, and other stats are reset upon exiting.
Guild members who joined the guild after matchmaking cannot enter the ongoing match.
Kill streak notifications remain displayed for 15 sec.
Equipment durability will not decrease during the match.
Participants are automatically placed into a platoon upon entry and removed upon exit.
If you are already in a party or platoon, you must leave it before entering.
Adventurers who joined a guild as a War Hero cannot view the Guild League menu or participate in Guild League.
Escape function is unavailable in the Guild League battlefield.
Consumables can be used without restrictions, except Medical Kits.
Deployable items (such as Stun Traps and Cannons) and Transformation Scrolls cannot be used.

Graphics Update
Here is an update on FSR 3.1 and DirectX 12, which many of you have been waiting for.
We had been developing these with the goal of applying them within August. However, given the wide variety of PC environments used by our Adventurers and the possibility of instability in certain configurations, we are conducting more careful, in-depth testing.
We are eager to bring you an improved environment as soon as possible, but we believe it should only be delivered once it is stable and polished enough to satisfy everyone who uses it, so the schedule has been delayed somewhat. Accordingly, we will prioritize FSR 3.1 and DirectX 12, followed by FSR 4.1 and DLSS.
We will share the schedule for these updates once we have reviewed the current state of development. We understand this may be disappointing news for Adventurers who have been waiting in anticipation. We will continue doing our utmost until the very end to deliver a stable, polished update.
