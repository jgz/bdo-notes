Improvements to Purified Lightstone Acquisition via Artifacts
Once Adventurers obtain all the Artifacts they need, they typically process any extras into Purified Lightstones via Simple Alchemy. With a steady supply of Artifacts, we expect more Adventurers to use Simple Alchemy for this purpose.
To reduce the hassle of converting Artifacts into Purified Lightstones, we have replaced Simple Alchemy with an NPC exchange. We have also increased the number of Purified Lightstones obtained to provide more opportunities to acquire Lightstones.
Removed the Simple Alchemy recipes that allowed you to obtain Purified Lightstones by processing the following artifacts, and changed the method so you can now exchange them with Dalishain instead.

Following this change, the item descriptions for Marsh's Artifacts, Lesha's Artifacts, Kehelle's Artifacts, and Sethra's Artifacts have been updated.
Previously, Simple Alchemy granted 1-2 Purified Lightstones. This has been changed so that exchanging now guarantees 2 Purified Lightstones.

Node & Conquest War
Conquest War Merit Reward System Improvements
The rules for Merit Rewards obtained from the Mediah and Valencia Conquest Wars have been adjusted.
Previously, rewards were distributed evenly based on the number of participating guild/alliance members. Under this system, an Adventurer with low Merit Points could receive a high reward if there were few participants, while someone with high Merit Points could receive a smaller reward if there were many participants.
To address this, we have improved the system so that guild/alliance members who earn more Merit Points receive appropriate rewards. Additionally, the maximum Merit Reward has been increased to 1.5 times the previous amount.
Adjusted the distribution rules for Merit Rewards obtainable based on the duration and performance of offensive and defensive sides in Mediah/Valencia Conquest Wars, and increased the Merit Reward amount.

Before: Distributed evenly (1/N) among participating guild/alliance members. (Merit Reward: 200 billion Silver)
After: Distributed at 1/100 among participating guild/alliance members. (Merit Reward: 300 billion Silver)
Merit Reward Distribution Rules
Defense Successful	The defensive guild/alliance receives the full Merit Reward. (Distributed at 1/100 among participating guild/alliance members.)
Defense Failed	The defensive guild/alliance receives a portion of the Merit Reward based on how long they defended. (Distributed at 1/100 among participating guild/alliance members.)
Offense Successful	The defensive guild/alliance's share (based on time defended) is deducted from the total. The remaining Merit Reward is distributed among the offensive guilds/alliances based on their performance.
Formula: Total Merit Reward - Defense Duration Reward = Remaining Merit Reward / Distributed to offensive guilds/alliances based on performance (each participating member receives a 1/100 share of the total).

* Rewards are not distributed to guilds/alliances with 0 Merit Point.
Terminology Definition
Defense Successful: When the defensive guild/alliance successfully destroys all offensive guild/alliance Command Posts/Field HQs, or when the defensive guild/alliance's Command Post/Field HQ remains standing until the end of the Conquest War.
Offense Successful: When the defensive guild/alliance's Command Post/Field HQ is destroyed before the Conquest War ends.

Guild Galley Ram Damage Adjustments
Ram is a ship skill that directly attacks an opponent's ship at the cost of your own ship's durability.
In the Blue Battlefield, which many Adventurers currently enjoy, the Guild Galley's Ram was overly powerful because, unlike cannon fire, its damage was unaffected by the number of members aboard. To encourage Guild Galleys to focus on cannon fire rather than ramming, we have reduced its damage. However, to compensate, we have also reduced the amount of durability consumed.
