New Additions and Improvements
This update focuses on adjusting classes that have been performing somewhat beyond the intended level of this balance patch, alongside structural improvements for certain classes.
We are also planning further adjustments for classes that remain underperforming after recent balance changes.
We will continue to review these changes closely to ensure the improvements are implemented reliably. Thank you.
Berserker
Berserker Awakening is a class that can deal high damage by repeatedly comboing skills into Flow: Split Shot. However, the long cooldowns on other skills made it difficult to maintain a smooth combo. Furthermore, the damage of the skills used to fill these gaps was lacking.
With this update, we are adding more options to combo into Flow: Split Shot, significantly reducing the cooldown of Flow: Ancient Wave, and increasing the damage of Devastation and Flow: Slugfest to help maintain his damage output even when Flow: Split Shot is unavailable.
Additionally, the defensive effect of Blasting has been improved to help him secure the All AP Increase effect more safely at the start of combat.

Edana Rising Token Improvement
The Edana Rising Tokens obtained from the [Weekly] For the Throne quests are used to challenge the Edana ruling each region of Edania.
Since these tokens may not be useful to Adventurers who do not wish to challenge the Edana, they were made sellable. However, because the tokens expire as part of the content's progression system, some Adventurers were unable to sell them before they expired.
To address this, we have improved the [Weekly] For the Throne quests so that Adventurers can now choose between an Edana Rising Token or Gold Bars as a reward.
For those who receive an Edana Rising Token but later decide to sell it, its sell price will remain unchanged.
Changed certain quest rewards for [Weekly] For the Throne to selectable rewards.

You can now choose to receive either the corresponding Edana Rising Token or Gold Bar 100G x3.
