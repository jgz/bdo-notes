# Hyperboost — research

## What it is

**Hyperboost** is a **catch-up / fast-track gear-progression system launched on NA/EU 2026-07-30**
(same patch as the new **Agent** class). It's aimed at **new AND returning players** and sits on
top of Season + Olvia Academy. Goal: condense what used to be **~40 days (or "the better part of a
year")** of gear progression into **~4 days** of focused play, landing a character around **~730–750
gearscore** with current top-tier gear:

- **3 Sovereign weapons** (main / sub / awakening) — the newest weapon tier, above Blackstar.
- **6 PEN (V) Kharazad accessories** — one per slot (2 rings, 2 earrings, necklace, belt).
- **4 Slumbering Origin armor pieces** — the current defense set.

A related buff shipped with it: **PEN (V) Tuvala gear now matches PEN (V) boss-gear stats** (was
~TET-equivalent before). [butwhytho](https://butwhytho.net/2026/08/black-desert-online-hyperboost-cost-how-to/),
[mmom](https://www.mmom.com/black-desert-news/detail_black-desert-hyperboost-season-graduation-guide.html)

## How it's structured

The canonical path is a **seasonal character**: **Season → graduation → Olvia Academy combat course
→ Hyperboost milestone quests.** Every enhancement milestone on the route (PEN Tuvala → PEN Blackstar
→ Sovereign; Kharazad; Slumbering Origin) has a **quest that directly hands you the core materials**,
plus **Obsidian Hammers** (guaranteed-stack items) to make the free PEN Blackstars.
[mmom](https://www.mmom.com/black-desert-news/detail_black-desert-hyperboost-season-graduation-guide.html),
[patched.gg](https://patched.gg/games/black-desert-online/july-30-hyperboost-and-new-class-agent-arrives)

## ⚠️ Does an existing main qualify, or must you roll a season character? (sources conflict)

This is the load-bearing question for Jon (existing Lv62 main, long since graduated season). Sources
**disagree**, so here's the reconciled read from the most reliable one (BDFoundry), with the conflict
noted:

- butwhytho says **"new characters only... automatically participating once they've completed Season
  and then Olvia Academy."**
- mmom says **"Hyperboost can be used on your main character, you don't need to be on a Season
  character... benefits apply to any character in your family."**
- **BDFoundry reconciles it** — most of it is **family-wide and doable on the main**, but the
  *efficient free PEN Blackstars* come from the season/Olvia run:

| Piece | On the existing main? | Requirement (per BDFoundry, 2026-08-21) |
|---|---|---|
| **PEN Kharazad accessories** (all 6) | ✅ Yes | **Once-per-Family** quests from **Alustin**; prereq = complete the **Olvia Academy Admission** quest. Family-wide. |
| **Slumbering Origin armor** (4) | ✅ Yes (with AP) | Black Spirit Support quests → beat **Black Shrine bosses solo (~305–315+ AP)**. |
| **Sovereign weapons** (3) | ◐ Limited | Convert from **PEN Blackstar** weapons. The *free* PEN Blackstars come from **Olvia Academy / (Y) challenge rewards** — i.e. most cheaply via a **season character**. On the main alone you'd buy PEN Blackstars from the market/bosses. |
| **Olvia Academy** progress | ✅ Yes | Family-wide. |

**Bottom line:** a returning main can grab the **Kharazad accessories** and **Slumbering Origin
armor** directly (huge upgrades).
[BDFoundry](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/new-player-guide/),
[butwhytho](https://butwhytho.net/2026/08/black-desert-online-hyperboost-cost-how-to/)

### ✅ Correction (2026-08-21): Sovereign weapons DON'T need a season character

Earlier I wrote that the free PEN Blackstars for Sovereign came cheapest via a season run. **That's
wrong** — the **Olvia Academy Combat Course**, which a **Lv60+ existing main enrolls in directly**,
grants **family-wide** rewards including a **"Choose Your PEN (V) Blackstar Weapon" box, 12 Obsidian
Hammers, and 6,000 Cron Stones** (plus Advice of Valks / Essence of Dawn). So the PEN Blackstar →
Sovereign pipeline runs entirely on the main. Jon confirmed in-game he sees the Hyperboost suggested
quests on Maegu and has the Olvia invitation.

**Olvia Academy is time-limited (semesters):** Class 3 = **2026-07-30 → 2026-11-05**. Course
*progress* + Academy Passes reset each semester; **Family Rewards (the gear) do not.** So claim the
Combat Course rewards before **2026-11-05**.

_(Reward figures vary by source — an earlier search said "up to 15,000 Crons" and a PEN+TET Blackstar
pair; BDFoundry lists 6,000 Crons + 12 hammers + one choose-PEN box. Confirm the exact current
rewards in-game. Also confirm how many free PEN Blackstars a non-season main nets across Academy +
Hyperboost, since 3 are needed for all weapon slots.)_
